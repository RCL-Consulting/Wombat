**Subject:** Paediatric EPA curriculum v11.1 — twelve short questions from the Wombat build

Dear colleagues,

Thank you for the answers to our September questions; every one of them is now built into Wombat. Building the rest of
the v11.1 programme raised twelve more. Each can be answered in a line. Where we have had to pick an interim answer,
we say what it is, so you only need to reply if it is wrong.

**The semester calendar and targets**

1. **June.** Is June itself in semester 1 or semester 2, or is the boundary a date in mid-June?
2. **December.** Which period does an encounter observed in December count towards?
3. **Per-semester figures.** Are Annexure B's per-semester figures a target *each* semester, or a planning split of the
   annual frequency? The pages for EPAs 3, 6 and 7 say "performed annually".
4. **Starting and finishing part-way.** Is a registrar who starts in the first days of a period (for example the first
   working day of January) exempt from that period's target? And is a registrar who finishes part-way through a period
   held to its target?
   *Interim:* a registrar who starts within a semester's first month is held to that semester's target, and one who
   starts later is exempt. A registrar who finishes before a semester's last month is exempt from it. For an
   academic-year target, only a start on or after 1 July exempts. An encounter observed after the last day counts for
   nothing.

**The assessment tools**

5. **"Clinical observed interaction".** Does it join Mini-CEX with Direct observation? If so, eight EPAs' tool lists
   change, and EPA 10 ("Leading and operating within a clinical team") becomes creditable by a Mini-CEX. Today they are
   separate tools.
8. **Chart-stimulated recall.** Is it the same instrument as Clinical Case Analysis? Today they are separate; a yes adds
   CCA to EPA 12 and chart-stimulated recall to EPAs 1, 3, 4 and 6.
9. **Learner feedback.** Is it multi-source feedback from students, junior trainees and team members? And is EPA 15's
   "at least two assessors across at least two teaching contexts" two separate occasions, or two respondent groups?
   *Interim:* recorded as multi-source feedback from learners; the count is reported to the committee, not enforced.
11. **The candidate's self-assessment (page 8).** Is there a prescribed form for the milestone self-reflection and
    learning plan? How often is it written, and who signs it off?
12. **Clinical audit on EPA 10.** Descriptor 10.5 says "Conduct a clinical audit", but Annexure A's tool list for EPA 10
    does not name the audit, so Wombat currently refuses an audit filed against EPA 10. Should the list include it?

**Entrustment decisions and committees**

6. **Committee structure.** Is the Clinical Competency Committee arrangement, including a neonatal CCC for EPAs 4 and 5,
   set by each institution, or mandated nationally?
   *Interim:* each institution sets up its own panels; EPAs 4 and 5 go to a neonatal panel where the institution has one.
10. **"Currency / status" (Annexure A).** Is the 6- or 12-month figure an expiry — an entrustment lapses if the EPA is
    not practised for that long — or the decision cadence that Annexure B relabels it as?
7. **Optional: MSF on the trajectory.** Should the level a releasing reviewer records on a multi-source feedback appear
   on a registrar's entrustment trajectory, or stay as evidence only? Today it is evidence only.
