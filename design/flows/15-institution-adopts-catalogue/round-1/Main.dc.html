9 · Edge cases flagged

  • A second discipline (Demo IM Core) beside Paediatrics: A1 and A2 list it as a separate row; it supersedes nothing, so it adopts with no question; its College's EPAs appear in C under the Demo College's name.
  • A College version closed (held back) after KGK adopted it: KGK keeps it; its row reads "Adopted on …" with no change; only new adoptions are refused.
  • A superseded version with no registrar left and no own item: it leaves Curricula's first table and stays in Adoption history only.
  • A move while the registrar's completion is being credited elsewhere: the save waits (T304); D's result still reads its counts; no new state drawn.
  • KGK's own EPA in a discipline KGK no longer adopts: it stays listed, Edit offered (it is KGK's), Create refused for that discipline (P7); its own items stay on the versions that hold them.
  • Two institutions' own EPAs with the same code on the Administrator's list: each row's owner names its institution (C1 by section heading, C2 by the badge), so "KGK-001" twice reads apart.
  • An EPA title of 150 characters wraps in its cell and in the h1; never cut (drawn on C1).
  • A registrar on 11.1 when KGK supersedes 11.2 with 11.3: the registrar stays on 11.1; his profile's picker offers 11.1 and 11.3 (the pin and the active adoption), never 11.2.

10 · Accessibility review (WCAG 2.1 AA), as drawn

  • One h1 per screen (PageHeader), h2 sections in order; a dialog's title is its h2.
  • Every table has a caption naming it ("EPAs your institution reads", "Items of Paediatric EPA Curriculum (11.2)").
  • Row actions are named for the row: "Edit KGK-001 — Running a paediatric outreach clinic at a district hospital", "Adopt Paediatric EPA Curriculum (11.2)…", "Remove KGK-001 — … from Paediatric EPA Curriculum (11.2)…".
  • Owner told in words (a section heading or the badge's words), never colour alone.
  • Focus: a dialog opens on Cancel; after confirm, the result alert (tabindex -1) takes the focus; after Cancel, the trigger gets it back; after a failed save, the refusal under its field (first in aria-describedby).
  • Targets: buttons 36px (row actions 28px) at 1280; 44px at 390, each section's action the row's width.
  • Contrast: only token pairs from DESIGN.md § Contrast; the badges' words are text-color on header-bg (11.36:1).
