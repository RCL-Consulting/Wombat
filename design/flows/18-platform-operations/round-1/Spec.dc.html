5 · PROPOSED (yours to rule on)

  1. B only: a System page, /admin/system, "System · Wombat", nav item Platform › System (first), trail Home › System.
  2. The institution record's h1 is its name, its tab "<name> · Wombat", its trail Home › Institutions › <name>; the create page keeps "Create institution" with a new subtitle.
  3. Status read out, with Deactivate… or Reactivate as its own action (no Active box). Reactivate asks nothing; its result "Institution reactivated." (today "Institution saved and reactivated." on Save).
  4. Result words: "‹key› is disabled. The scheduler will not run it until you enable it."; "‹key› is enabled. Its next run is ‹time›."; "‹key› ran and succeeded. It started at ‹time› and took ‹n› ms. What it did is written to the application log."; "‹key› ran and failed. … Its error: …". The refusal's words are kept.
  5. While Run now waits, its button reads "Running…" (aria-disabled, with a status line).
  6. A schedule in words and SAST ("Daily at 09:00 SAST", "Hourly, on the hour", "Mondays at 10:00 SAST"); the raw cron is not shown.
  7. "Job run history" is renamed "Run history" (the header link's words); its key box becomes a select of the eight keys; "Triggered by" becomes "Ran by" (a name, or Scheduler); the Finished column folds into Started + Duration.

  11. Institutions' Created shows the date only.

8 · Accessibility review (WCAG 2.1 AA)

  • One h1 per page; h2 below it (Home's card titles, the lists' "Runs"/"Actions", the entry's and record's sections); no h3 under an h1.
  • Every table has a caption ("8 jobs", "2 institutions", "Runs, newest first", "Actions, newest first").
  • No state in colour alone: every badge carries its word; Home's dots go.
  • Row actions named for their row: "Run now: assessor-pending-nudge", "Disable: …", "Edit Kgosi Kgari Teaching Hospital", "Detail: CreateActivityCommand at 19:01:41".
  • An unavailable Run now is aria-disabled with its reason as text, tied by aria-describedby.
  • Focus: a result takes it (ActionResult); Show moves it to the count line; Previous/Next to the list's h2; the dialog opens on Cancel and returns to the result; a refused form to its first refused field; the way back to the Detail link left.
  • Refusals tied to their fields (aria-invalid, aria-describedby, refusal first).
  • Targets: 28 px row buttons at 1280 (≥24); 44 px and full width at 390 (people pages' rule, R9).
  • The payload region is focusable (tabindex 0), named "Payload", scrolls on its own; nothing on any page scrolls sideways at 390.
  • Text on tints: badge words are body text on the tint (T322); muted text 5.09:1 on white.
