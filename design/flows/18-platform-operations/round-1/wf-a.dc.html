Structure A · Home holds it all — no System page, no nav item

Reasoning. The operator comes rarely and lands on Home, so Home answers the one question they arrive with: is Wombat healthy, and if not, where do I act. Three lines in words (Database, Mail, Scheduled jobs) and the Accounts card fit one screen at 1280 and two thumbs at 390. Detail already has a home: each job's line is Scheduled jobs, each run is Run history. Curriculum progress keeps its way in from Curricula's header (flow 15) and the operator's own account its way in from the name in the top bar (flow 12), so nothing new is needed to find them. Fixed order (Database, Mail, Jobs) so a returning eye finds a line where it was.

What it changes. Home's Administrator cards only (allowed). § Status dots: the dot goes, the word stays. § Dashboard page: health spans two columns, Accounts one. Nothing PROPOSED in the shell; A.5.13 holds. DATA: the mail figures, the account breakdown.
