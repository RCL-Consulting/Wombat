Why this structure

  • It is the pattern Wombat already teaches: a Home card of five with "Open …", a list page that is the whole truth (flow 04). Staff learn nothing new.
  • Home stays one screen a card whatever the programme's size: five rows, a count, a way on. The heavy state lives on pages built for it (pager, filters, stacked rows).
  • Triage sits in one place that can filter by assessor, so "chase Mohammed Patel about his three" is one filtered view, not three trips.
  • Cost: two pages and three menu items more than B; the Coordinator's menu is full at 8.
