Why, and what it costs

  • Home answers "is anything wrong?" in three numbers on one phone screen; the detail is one press away, always in the same place.
  • The By EPA grid answers the brief's first two questions at once: which EPAs, and which registrars on each. Neither A nor B shows that crossing.
  • Cost: one press more on every first look. A figure with no names asks to be opened.
  • Cost: two NEW components (ViewTabs, ProgrammeGrid) and a definition to settle: a registrar "met every target" counts both windows, so "0 of 5" will read 0 for most of a semester. A threshold for "behind" (e.g. short with 30 days left) is a domain rule the operator would have to set.
