Accessibility review, WCAG 2.1 AA, against all three

  • Names (2.4.4, 4.1.2). Every registrar link is her name; every EPA link "PAED-001 — Providing paediatric emergency care to children"; every activity link flow 03's "Mini-CEX (Paediatrics) · PAED-004 · 2026-09-30, from Nomsa Mahlangu" (T280). "Send a reminder" names its row: "Send Thandi Zulu a reminder about Mini-CEX (Paediatrics) · PAED-004 · 2026-09-30".
  • No card is one link around links (T280): C's figure cards hold one link each and nothing else interactive.
  • Figures as words (1.1.1, 1.4.1). Every bar sits under its words ("2 of 5 registrars met"), aria-hidden; the grid's cells say "0 of 3, short" or "Met", never a mark alone; Overdue is a word beside the state.
  • Targets (2.5.5 as the brief sets it). Rows 44 px at every width (they are lists of links); buttons 36 px, 44 px at 390; folds' summaries 44 px.
  • Focus (2.4.3, 3.3.1). Try again's answer focuses the first card's heading, or the empty state; after a reminder the result alert takes the focus, and the next Tab is the next row's link; a refusal takes it the same way. A filter's Show puts it on the table's heading with its count.
  • Scroll regions. C's grid at 1280 past eight registrars scrolls inside a labelled, focusable region with the ring (T328); nothing at 390 scrolls sideways.
