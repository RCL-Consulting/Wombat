Flags for the build

  • Revoke the STAR enables as the reason is typed (T260): @bind:event="oninput", one SignalR round trip a keystroke while the dialog is open. The alternative is always enabled, with “Give a reason for the revocation.” under the box.
  • FilterBar's word is “Show”; C24 keeps “Apply filters” here. One of the two should give way.
  • The Issued link assumes register scope and review-read scope coincide for these four roles; otherwise it is plain text.
  • “Appeal resolved.” lands ~7,800 px below the top at 1280; the result takes the focus, so the page scrolls to it.

Edge cases

  • An entrustment-only review appealed and remitted: no category, no STAR changes (2 c; 3 b).
  • A panel that cannot seat a quorum; an appeal body nobody can act for; a member who cannot act (3 a, c).
  • A STAR revoked on a paused EPA: its row reads Revoked with “(no longer in use)”; it moves to No longer in force with its paused mark.
  • A superseded STAR beside its successor: the line names the successor; superseded never shows on My authorisations.
  • A graduate: My authorisations goes with the Trainee role (flow 13's); not drawn.
  • The 120-character title (PAED-015) wraps in cards, rows and stacked blocks.
  • A 4,000-character reason: six lines, then “Show the whole reason” (aria-expanded), on both sides.

Accessibility (WCAG 2.1 AA)

  • One h1 a page; each section an h2 (Decision, Appeal, Agenda, Appeals, In force, No longer in force); a decision and an appeal entry an h3; dialogs their own h2.
  • Every table captioned; headers scope="col", the row's subject scope="row"; stacked cells keep their labels as generated content with empty alt.
  • Every row action named for its row: View, Download, Revoke; badges are words.
  • Focus: the h1 on arrival; the field on a blank reason; the safe button in Lodge, Remit and Dismiss dialogs; the reason box in Revoke; each result and refusal in an ActionResult (tabindex −1); Go back and Keep editing return it to their opener.
  • Targets: 24px at 1280 and 768; 44px at 390 (.outcomes scope); ticks 2.75rem rows.
  • The quorum line is aria-live polite, the one live region besides results.
