# F20 The PDFs Wombat prints: the portfolio, the STAR certificate and the data-export summary

Three documents leave Wombat as PDFs. A registrar's portfolio goes to her next employer. A STAR certificate is the one
document per EPA that records what she is entrusted with. A data-export summary answers a data-subject access request.
They are the record that leaves the product: a stranger reads them with no account, in print or on screen. This flow
designs all three, and the frame they share.

Part of the GUI redesign briefed in `design/BRIEF.md` (W-008, T336). Written 2026-09-27.

**The certificate, as printed** (`EntrustmentCertificatePdfService.cs:35-210`):
- **Header:** the institution, "Statement of Awarded Responsibility", "Entrustment Decision" and a rule.
- **The statement:** "This certifies that <name> is authorised to perform the following EPA". It reads the same for
  every status.
- **A bordered box:** the EPA code and title, "Authorised level: <label>" and the level's description.
- **Three columns:** Issued on, Expires on ("No expiry"), and Status. The status is its enum name, coloured green, red,
  orange or grey.
- **The body:** the rationale; the evidence summary (each linked activity, its state, EPA, rating, encounter date and
  "updated … UTC"); Issued by (panel, "Committee review: #n", chair); a rule over "Chair signature on file".
- **Revoked only:** a red box reading "REVOKED", the day, the revoker's user id and the reason.
- **Footer:** "Decision #n". It has no page count and no verification line.

## Questions the design must answer

1. **The frame.** A real first page and a slim running header, or today's repeated block? Step 5.11 expects "every page
   carries the same header and footer"; if the design changes it, the step's Expect changes with it (BRIEF § 9, item
   7).
2. **How a reader verifies.** A PDF cannot print its own SHA-256. The choices are to print the verify address with
   "SHA-256 of this file", or to print a short export code that `/portfolio/verify` also accepts. The second is NEW,
   and the verify page would change with T313 (F13).
3. **Certificates.** Should a certificate be verifiable too? Its hash is recorded nowhere (BRIEF § 7 B10). If yes, the
   footer and the verify page need it.
4. **Whose document it is.** KGK's, the CPSA's or Wombat's? Does it carry the institution's logo, which the data model
   holds and nothing draws?
5. **The MSF.** Should the portfolio print it once, not twice?
6. **The data-export summary.** Is it the portfolio relabelled, or a readable rendering of everything held (the JSON
   beside it)? Should it be verifiable, and should it say which request it answers?
7. **Type.** Keep Lato, or embed the product's faces? The body font is still open (BRIEF § 4.6), and the wordmark is
   Fraunces (OFL).

## Notes

- **A PDF is not a page.** BRIEF § 5.1's Blazor, CSP and component rules do not apply inside the document; only its
  Content and Accessibility lines do. The pages that produce and check the PDFs (Export, My authorisations, Entrustment
  decisions, Data rights, Verify) are designed in F13, F09 and F14.
- **Why the PDFs carry no time.** Identical data must give identical bytes (T078), so three exports of one record
  verify as one file (Steps 5.9, 5.10 and 5.12). A design that adds "Printed on …" breaks it; one that prints the
  period, the programme dates and the decision dates does not.
- **The certificate's statuses** are Active, Superseded, Revoked and Expired (`EntrustmentDecisionStatus`). Superseded
  means a later decision on the same EPA replaced it: PAED-010's level 4 was replaced by level 5 at the pre-graduation
  review.
