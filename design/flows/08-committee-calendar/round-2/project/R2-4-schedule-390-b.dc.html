Put a trainee before a panel for a period. The review opens once it is created.

Only the panels with a trainee you can schedule are listed.
Current registrars at the panel's institution whose programme you oversee.
The semester the committee decides for. Choosing one fills the evidence window from the start of its academic year, so the annual EPAs a semester-2 sitting decides are judged on the whole year.The College's semester 2 ends in November; reviews for it may sit until 31 December.
