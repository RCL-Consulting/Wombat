Accessibility review, WCAG 2.1 AA

  • One h1 a page, from PageHeader; then h2 for each section (By EPA, Decisions, Agenda, Who decides each EPA, About this panel, Who sits on it, Decides for), h3 below (the curriculum's name). The sitting bar's h2 is flow 07's, visually hidden.
  • Every table has a caption: Decision panels at Kgosi Kgari Teaching Hospital; Who decides each EPA of Paediatric EPA Curriculum 11.1; Seats on the panel…; By EPA's; Decisions due in 2026 S2…; Lerato Molefe's decisions due…; Committee reviews at Kgosi Kgari Teaching Hospital…
  • Every row action is named for its row: “Schedule a review of Lerato Molefe for PAED-004 before Paed Annual Review Panel”, “Open review #1 for Lerato Molefe, PAED-001”, “Edit Paed Annual Review Panel”, “Open the 2026 S2 annual progression review of Lerato Molefe before Paed Annual Review Panel”. A row with no action says why: “Decided: nothing to schedule”.
  • Badges read as words; the tint repeats them (Decided green, Not decided amber, Revoked and Missed red, Scheduled blue, the rest grey; review states per StateBadge).
  • The pager's select is named “Decisions per page” by a <label for> (T280); the pager is a landmark, “Decisions, pages”.
  • Focus: after Show or a page turn, the Decisions heading (“Decisions, page 2 of 4”); after Create panel, Save panel and Save committee, the result; after a refusal, its summary; on the schedule page, the h1 on arrival, and the Already held warning is announced (aria-live) without moving the focus; after Create review, the review's h1; after Try again, the alert again or the content's heading. Every focus target stays clear of the top bar (scroll-padding-top).
  • Fields: every control has a <label for>; help and messages linked by aria-describedby; the Trainee select's disabled state is explained in its help; a refused field is marked by border and stripe, a refused group by the stripe, never colour alone.
  • Radios: each seat and each review type is a native radio in a fieldset whose legend names it (“Seat for Sarah Botha”, visually hidden; “Review type”).
  • Targets: buttons 36px, small buttons 28px, radio rows 28px from 641px; every target 44px below 641px (the rows' actions, Show, Clear filters, the fold, the seat choices, the type choices, the pager).
  • 390px: no page scrolls sideways (each board was checked at its width); By EPA stacks; tables stack; the 120-character EPA title wraps.
  • Reduced motion: the fold's chevron and the skeletons stop (bundle.css's rule).

Edge cases

  • An Administrator, who belongs to no institution: Decisions due and Decision panels wait for an institution (frames drawn).
  • A panel of two (the chair and one member): accepted; the seat count reads “2 seats: the chair, 1 member and 0 external members.”
  • A panel sitting as the neonatal committee: PAED-004 and 005 route to it; a registrar's decisions then sit before two panels, each with its own seat (heavy frames).
  • A registrar whose review is already held: Already held before Create; the row's one action is Open review #n.
  • A formative check-in on the same period: accepted, takes no seat; the trainee line says so.
  • A registrar who has graduated or withdrawn: not offered in Trainee (5.29), not listed on Decisions due.
  • A deleted user's pseudonym: shown as stored in Committee reviews (flow 14's).
  • The 120-character EPA title: wraps in the row header and the Agenda, never cut.

Flags and PROPOSED

  • Show, not Apply (C9). FilterBar names the button Show on every list (flow 06, review 33: one button word for every list), so it is drawn Show. One word to change if Apply is wanted.
  • PAED-001's title is the brief's (“Managing common paediatric emergencies”); the design system's previews say “Providing paediatric emergency care to children”. Other titles are placeholders.
  • Windows: PAED-001 to 006 are drawn as semester EPAs, the other nine annual (the brief does not say which six).
  • Placeholders: the neonatal panel's name, the Administrator's name, and a speciality admin elsewhere (nowhere to create).
  • Nav icons for MSF campaigns, Trainees, Assessors and Curriculum adoptions are taken from NavMenu's icon list by guess; the build uses NavItems.cs's.
  • PROPOSED, not built: dates typed as ISO text; a lone Speciality option preselected; no Review type preselected (a refusal names it); the trainee line; the By EPA fold; the Decides for section offering nothing while a review is open; the words of new results (“… was created. Its reviews can now be scheduled on Committee reviews.”, the seat count).
