DESIGN.md: rule text that changes

SectionNew text
§ Committee agendaThe agenda is the review page's spine: one line per EPA due in the window, then the chair's lines. A line opens in place to stage a decision; a staged decision is shown in its line, not in a separate list. There is no deferral (D53): a line due at this sitting that is not staged when the decision is recorded becomes "Not decided at this sitting", with the decision's optional sentence under it. The caption counts what is staged ("15 EPAs for 2026 S2 · 3 staged").
§ The chair's controlsThe chair's acts live in the sitting bar (Start, Go to the decision, Ratify, Close review) and in the agenda's lines (Stage, Remove); everyone else reads the who-may-act sentence in the bar's place, and the controls are not drawn. Record is held back only by the quorum. Start is not offered while the chair cannot act (T260).
§ Entrustment-only reviewsThe decision form has no Category; the record reads "Entrustment decisions only"; "staged below" becomes "staged above".
§ Detail page and its details-gridThe committee review is the one detail page with no details-grid: one column in a fixed order (sitting bar, notes, agenda, decision, standing, MSF, snapshot, trajectories, appeals); the decision card rises above the agenda once recorded.
§ Page-level patterns · the title ruleA record page's h1 names its subject: "Lerato Molefe · 2026 S2", and the tab "Lerato Molefe · 2026 S2 · Wombat" (T190); while loading, "Committee review".
The owner table, a committee reviewUnchanged route; Committee reviews lights for every role that reaches it. The activity page opened from a review keeps its own owner and gains the return link.
