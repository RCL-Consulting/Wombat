DESIGN.md: rule text that changes

SectionNew text
§ Committee agendaThe agenda is the review page's spine: one line per EPA due in the window, then the chair's lines. One line opens at a time to stage a decision; a staged decision is shown in its line. There is no deferral (D53): every line left unstaged when the review is ratified reads "Not decided at this sitting", with the decision's optional sentence. The caption counts what is staged.
§ The chair's controlsThe chair's acts live in her sticky sitting bar and in the agenda's lines; readers see the who-may-act sentence, controls not drawn. Record is held back only by the quorum. Start is offered to the chair, members who may sit, the Coordinator and the Administrator, never while the chair cannot act.
§ Page-level patterns · page actions"The primary page action lives in the PageHeader action slot" gains: except on the committee review, where it lives in the sitting bar.
§ Page-level patterns · the title ruleA record page's h1 names its subject: "Lerato Molefe · 2026 S2" (tab "… · Wombat"); "Committee review" while loading.
§ Entrustment-only reviewsNo Category; recording needs at least one staged decision; the record reads "Entrustment decisions only"; "staged below" becomes "staged above".
§ Detail page and its details-gridThe committee review has no details-grid: one column, the decision card rising above the agenda once recorded.
§ Stacking (new).sidebar z-index: 10 at 641 px and wider; sticky page bars z-index: 1; scroll-padding-top covers the top bar and any stuck page bar.
