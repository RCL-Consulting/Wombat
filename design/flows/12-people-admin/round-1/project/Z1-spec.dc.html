DESIGN.md rules each changes

  • § Table system — all Users variations: roles printed by label with their scope under or beside (“Committee member · Paediatrics”), never keys; an Institution column only for a caller whose reads span institutions; Users and Assessors stack from 900 px (StackWide). UB replaces the table with rows (a list page not built on DataTable: a rule change).
  • § Form system — S1/S2: a form inside a detail page’s section (Add a role, Assessor training) opened in place, its result in the section; Add a role asks the scope its role takes by Invite a person’s rules (PROPOSED). S3 keeps the form a page of its own.
  • § Button system, the destructive action — every page here: Remove…, Lock out… and Delete… are outline triggers named for their row; the filled danger button lives only in the ConfirmDialog, which names the person and role (or group and role). No destructive button on a record already in that state (a locked person shows Reactivate only).
  • § Page-level patterns — Detail page: the user page is one column of sections, Roles first (S1), or tabs (S2). Title rule: her institution’s page is titled by its name (h1, tab and record crumb), PROPOSED; the Administrator’s view keeps “Edit institution” (flow 18). List page: Assessors lists every Assessor holder (S1/S2).
  • § Dashboard page — Home counts roles by label and what KGK has adopted (1 and 1); Quick links dropped in H1 and H2 (the menu holds the four), kept in H3; a waiting line (H1) or Needs you card (H2) when registrars wait.
  • § Alerts — results name the person (“Assessor added to Thandi Zulu …”) and take the focus; the waiting line is an info .alert-row with its link, not an alert that stays.

PROPOSED (rule changes for you to accept)

  • ConfirmDialog for Lock out (naming the person and listing what applies to that person’s roles), Remove a role, and Delete a mapping (T264). Reactivate stays one press.
  • Results naming the person: “Assessor added to Thandi Zulu, in Paediatrics / Paediatrics.”, “Assessor removed from Thandi Zulu.”, “Password reset for Pieter du Plessis.”, “Mohammed Patel is locked out.”, “Mohammed Patel is reactivated and can sign in again.”, “Kgosi Kgari Teaching Hospital saved.”, “Assessor training saved for Fatima Khumalo: …”.
  • Pending invitations card and “Revoke all pending invitations” dropped from the user page (Q9).
  • Institutional sign-in links listed on the user page with Remove… (T286, Q10); a password reset does not remove them.
  • S1/S2: assessor training recorded on the person’s page; Assessors lists every Assessor holder with “Not recorded” until done; /admin/assessors/edit redirects to the person’s page at the section; the profile records training only and the Assessor role owns the discipline (Q2, Q12).
  • Assessor training kept when Assessor is removed, hidden until Assessor is given back (edge case).
  • Users: filter applied with Show (no per-keystroke filtering), Role and Status filters (UA), a pager over 25, no Institution column for her; header action Invite a person (opens flow 11’s page).
  • Home: a waiting line or Needs you card linking to Trainees (Q8); Your institution card (Q3); Users card renamed People with roles by label, each a link to Users filtered to it (H1).

DATA (a page would need what it does not read today)

  • Users: each role’s scope (speciality, sub-speciality) per row; a role filter; a page of 25 with the total.
  • User page: each role’s scope; the person’s institutional sign-in links (provider, linked on); requests waiting for the person (for the lock and the Remove Assessor confirmations).
  • Assessors (S1/S2): every Assessor holder at the institution, joined to the training record if any.
  • Home: count of Pending trainees waiting; locked accounts; adopted specialities and sub-specialities at her institution (T291 item 4); the institution’s name, short code, contact email.
  • Home H2: Assessors with no training recorded.
  • SSO: providers configured for her institution.

Edge cases

  • A 60-character address: wraps anywhere in its column at 1280 (.person-address overflow-wrap) and in the stacked block at 390; never cut. Drawn in round 2’s heavy board.
  • A person holding four roles: four lines in Roles and scope, each with its own Remove…; on Users four .role-cell lines.
  • Her own account: read only, the note first (T278); her row on Users has Manage like any other.
  • An Administrator’s account opened by id: “User unavailable”, as any out-of-scope id (A.5.5).
  • A locked person who is the assessor of a request waiting: the confirmation counts it [DATA]; whether the request moves is a rule for you (Q, not drawn as settled).
  • A person with no role (D54): Roles and scope says so and why nothing shows for them; Add a role offered (staff roles), Trainee never.
  • An assessor whose Assessor role is removed: the training record kept, hidden (PROPOSED); the Assessors list drops the person.
  • A Speciality admin added with no speciality: refused under Speciality, “Choose the speciality this role keeps.” (PROPOSED refusal).
  • Two people with one name: Users shows each address under the name; Manage’s accessible name adds the address only when names repeat (“Manage Sarah Botha, botha@…”).
  • The Demo Institution’s id: flow 01’s Page not found, unchanged; no record named.
  • A provider configured for another institution: not offered in her Provider select; her page reads “not set up” if none is hers.

Accessibility review (WCAG 2.1 AA), round 1

  • One h1 per page; sections are h2 (T280): the user page’s cards were h3 under the h1.
  • Every table captioned, and no caption repeats the h1 (“At Kgosi Kgari Teaching Hospital, by last name …”).
  • Every row action named for its row: “Manage Thandi Zulu”, “Remove Assessor from Thandi Zulu”, “Edit training for Fatima Khumalo”, “Delete the mapping of [group] to Assessor”. UB’s action is the name link itself.
  • Focus: Show → the list heading (UA, UB) or the match line (UC); every result → its Alert; a ConfirmDialog opens on its safe button and returns focus to its trigger on cancel; a refusal → the first refused field.
  • Refusals tied by aria-describedby and aria-invalid; an invalid field has the stripe, not colour alone; the password field is never redrawn with what was typed.
  • Targets: 24 px at 1280 (btn-sm 28); 44 px at 390 on every button, link row, radio row and filter control.
  • Contrast: only system tokens; the Locked out badge is text on danger-bg (11.81:1), its edge 5.56:1.
