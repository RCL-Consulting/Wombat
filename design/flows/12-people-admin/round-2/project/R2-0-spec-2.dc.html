For the operator: PROPOSED notes

  • (a) The product stores no pronoun, so Q13’s and C13’s sentences say “they” and “their”, as the built lockout list does.
  • (b) IdentityUserLogin stores no date, and flow 02’s My account shows the provider’s name and “Institutional sign-in.”, so each link reads its provider’s name with no “linked on”. A date would need a migration.
  • (c) Add a role’s Assessor writes the sub-speciality at 2.13, so Zulu’s, Naidoo’s and Botha’s scope is Paediatrics / Paediatrics from 2.13, not from 2.14’s save. A Committee member may not be invited with a sub-speciality, but holding both roles, the one scope serves both, as today after 2.14.
  • (d) An account with no institution reads “Global” on Users and “Global (no institution)” on the user page today: one wording, “Global (no institution)”, NEW (U14).
  • (e) The training section’s refusals and result are NEW words, and its status has no default (the captures show Not started preselected today).
  • (f) Back to users goes: the trail carries it (Q1). At 390 the trail folds to its 44 px parent link.

Accessibility (WCAG 2.1 AA)

  • One h1 per page; sections h2, parts h3 (T280). Every table captioned, and no caption repeats the h1. Headers carry scope.
  • Every row action is named for its row: “Manage Thandi Zulu” (the address too when names repeat, T239), “Edit training for Thandi Zulu”, “Remove Assessor from Thandi Zulu”, “Delete the mapping of ‹group› to Assessor”, “Remove the ‹provider› sign-in from Thandi Zulu”.
  • Focus after each action. Show goes to the list’s heading and count. A result goes to its alert in its section. A refusal goes to its field, tied by aria-describedby and aria-invalid. A dialog opens on its safe button, and Cancel or Escape returns to the trigger. A disclosure goes to its first control on open and back to the trigger on Cancel.
  • A disabled action is aria-disabled and says why in visible text (the last way in). An invalid field gets the stripe, not colour alone. A password is never drawn.
  • Targets: btn-sm 28 px at 1280; at 390 every button, radio row, link row and filter control is 44 px, and stacked actions put the primary on top.
