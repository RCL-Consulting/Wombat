  • § Table system. Add: “A role is printed by its label on every page. A person has one scope, shown once in its own column (Users) or in the who line (the user page), never beside each role. Users, Assessors and SSO mappings stack from 900 px (StackWide). An Institution column shows only to a caller whose reads span institutions.”
  • § Form system. Add: “A form inside a detail page’s section opens in place as a disclosure: a trigger with aria-expanded and aria-controls, the panel under it with a 4 px secondary-color stripe; the focus goes to the panel’s first control on open and back to the trigger on Cancel; on success the section’s result replaces the panel and takes the focus. A scope already held or the only one adopted is drawn as text, not a select.”
  • § Button system, the destructive action. Replace the user page’s filled Remove and Lock out user: “Remove…, Lock out… and Delete… are outline triggers named for their row (aria-haspopup=dialog). The filled danger button is only in the ConfirmDialog, which names the person and the role (or the group and the role) and opens on its safe button. A record already in that state shows no destructive button (a locked person shows Reactivate user).”
  • § Page-level patterns. Detail page: “The user page is one column of sections under h2, each result in its section.” Title rule: “Her institution’s page is titled by its name: h1, tab and record crumb (Home › Kgosi Kgari Teaching Hospital); nothing is lit.” List page: “Assessors lists the holders of the Assessor role, joined to their training.” The owner table gains: /admin/users/{UserId} under Users; /admin/institutions/{Id} under Home for an Institutional admin.
  • § Dashboard page. “Counts are by label; the curriculum card counts what the institution has adopted; a waiting line above the cards shows only while someone waits; Quick links are dropped where the menu has them.”
  • § Alerts. “A result names the person or the record: ‘Assessor added to Thandi Zulu.’, ‘Mohammed Patel is locked out.’, ‘Kgosi Kgari Teaching Hospital saved.’”
