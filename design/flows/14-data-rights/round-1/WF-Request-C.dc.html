Structure C — a next-step card leads; the rest is the record

Under the header, one status card says where the request stands and what is next, with that one action (the activity page's status card, flow 03): "Approved: make each correction, record it, then complete." Its steps tick as they are done. Then the record in one column: Request, Decision, Corrections. Record a correction… opens a note panel in place under the list.

Every state of a request reads the same way: Submitted ("Decide: approve or reject with a note"), Approved, Completed, Rejected, Withdrawn ("Withdrawn by Pieter du Plessis before a decision. Nothing to do.").

Changes and why

  • NEW component: RequestStatusCard (DashboardCard frame, emphasis tint), in place of a Decision card that only appears once decided.
  • The record chosen from a select of places (DATA as B); its owning page linked from the panel when the caller may open it (Account rows: none for staff).
  • PROPOSED: Q1's role; the refusal; Complete held in the status card, so it is reached first.

Steps (presses + fields typed), from the queue's row

  • Carry a correction through: 5 + 2 at 1280 (Review; Record a correction…; choose the record; before, after typed; Record; Complete request). 5 + 2 at 390.
  • Decide (A.1.3): 2 + 1 after the row, the note in the card.
