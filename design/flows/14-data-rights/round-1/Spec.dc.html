Edge cases flagged

  • A requester with no institution: in no Coordinator's queue; the Administrator's reads Institution "None". Her form says "The Wombat administrator decides it" in place of the institution.
  • A staff member's own request in the queue he reviews (Pieter Smit asks for his own export): he may not decide it. PROPOSED: the row shows, Decide is replaced by "You cannot decide your own request; another Coordinator or the Administrator does." (a new refusal).
  • An erasure of a person with a review under appeal: kept and answerable; the dialog lists it under Kept ("1 review under appeal, still answerable").
  • PseudonymSalt missing, or a concurrent change: drawn on Decide V1; nothing erased, the request still Submitted, the note kept.
  • Withdrawn while its page is open elsewhere: the next press is refused in words (drawn), and the page reloads as Withdrawn.
  • An export whose PDF fails: the zip holds the JSON only, saying nothing. Not this flow's (flow 20's export); flag: the download should say so.
  • A correction to an ended, read-only programme: the owning page refuses the change; the Correction section says "Her programme has ended: its record cannot be changed. Reject, or record what was changed elsewhere."
  • Two requests of one type on one day: each named by its time to the minute; two in the same minute differ only by id on the queue; on My data rights they read alike (both rows shown, newest first).
  • A 60-character address: drawn on both queues; wraps at the @ and anywhere after.
  • A reason of 4,000 characters: the row on My data rights shows the first 200 and "Show the whole reason" (round 2), the request page the whole of it with pre-line.
  • Access and Export build the same zip today; the form's help must not promise a difference. Yours to say whether they stay two types.
  • Account › Last name: no staff page changes a name today (only My account). A correction to a name therefore waits on the person, or needs a staff edit (out of this flow).
