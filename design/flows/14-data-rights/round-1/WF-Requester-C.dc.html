Structure C — a card per request, the form opened in place

Each request is a card of its own (an <h3> under Your requests): what she asked, when, its status, then The answer as a quoted block, and its one action. A legal answer read carefully gets room, not a table cell. Make a request… opens the form in a note panel in place (flow 12's Add a role… pattern); Cancel folds it.

Order: Your requests, then Reminder emails. With no request the panel's button is the first thing she meets.

Changes and why

  • No table on her page: a list of cards, so nothing has to stack or scroll at 390 (T323), and her reason is shown back to her.
  • The form behind one press: the page reads as her record, not a form (Q4).
  • Reminder emails: the checkbox reads "Stop the regular reminder emails" (changes A.2.3/A.2.5's Do).
  • DATA: decision date and note on each card, as in A.

Steps (presses + fields typed)

  • Export and download: 5 + 1 at 1280 (link, Make a request…, Export, reason, Submit; later Download); 6 + 1 at 390.
  • Read a rejected answer: 1 + 0.
  • Opt out of the digest: 3 + 0; 4 + 0 at 390.
  • Withdraw: 1 + 0, on the card.
