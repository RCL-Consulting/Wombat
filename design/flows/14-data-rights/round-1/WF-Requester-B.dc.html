Structure B — her list is the page; the form is a page of its own

Flow 11's rule: the list is the page and its create form a page of its own. My data rights holds Your requests and Reminder emails; the header action Make a request opens /account/data-rights/new (PROPOSED route), whose trail is Home › My data rights › Make a request. Submit returns to the list with the result focused over the new row.

The form page has room to say what each type does and what happens next, at length, without pushing her answers down. Cancel returns with nothing sent.

Changes and why

  • A new route and page title "Make a request · Wombat" (PROPOSED); DESIGN.md owner table: My data rights owns it.
  • The answer as a second line of each row, not a column (fits 1280 without a wide Answer column; stacks at 390 as one block). DATA as in A.
  • A.1.2's Expect changes: the refusal is on the form page; the result on the list.
  • Reminder emails as in A.

Steps (presses + fields typed)

  • Export and download: 5 + 1 at 1280 (link, Make a request, Export, reason, Submit; later Download); 6 + 1 at 390.
  • Read a rejected answer: 1 + 0.
  • Opt out of the digest: 3 + 0; 4 + 0 at 390.
  • Cost: one press more than A to make a request; a page load between.
