Structure A — one page, her requests first

One page, three <h2> sections in this order: Your requests (each with its answer), Make a request, Reminder emails. A returning requester came to read an answer, so the answer comes first; a first-timer sees an empty list that points down to the form.

The type is a choice of four described radios (flow 08's choice-group), so no gloss is cut off. The answer is a column of the table: the decision, its date, the institution's note. Never who decided.

Changes and why

  • Order and words of the requester's page (T280: one h1, then h2s; T318: the optional-processing flag gone).
  • A decided row shows its answer (T319). DATA: the row reads DecidedAt and DecisionNote, which the summary does not carry today.
  • Times in SAST with the zone (T325).
  • Submitted result: "Its answer shows here once it is decided." The email line stays only if Q5's notice is built (PROPOSED).
  • Preferences part renamed "Reminder emails"; its two results differ (stopped / sent again). Changes A.1.1, A.2.3, A.2.5's Expects.

Steps (presses + fields typed)

  • Export and download: 4 + 1 at 1280 (menu link, Export, reason, Submit; later Download); 5 + 1 at 390 (Menu first).
  • Read a rejected answer: 1 + 0 (the menu link; the answer is on the row). Today it cannot be read at all.
  • Opt out of the digest: 3 + 0 (link, tick, Save); 4 + 0 at 390.
  • Withdraw: 1 + 0 from the page, no dialog (Q14).
