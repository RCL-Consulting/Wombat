2 · DESIGN.md rules, ready to paste

R1 § Dashboard page. The Assessor's Home is two cards: "Waiting for you" (the inbox's first five rows, from the same read and in the same order and words; the count in words; no "0" badge; "Open Activity inbox") and "Recent decisions" (five, dated, each linked by its full name; "All your decisions"). A Home may carry one line about another held role's waiting work, only when work waits there; the line holds no switch.

R2 § Page-level patterns, List page. A list page may hold two headed, counted sections, the second paged. The Activity inbox stacks its rows below 641 px; its Waiting column replaces Updated ("8 days", "since … SAST", "Less than a day"). ActivityLink's second line reads "from <registrar>" in the assessor's lists.

R3 § Page-level patterns, Record page with a workflow. The result may carry the way on (WayOn); the live region holds the result's sentence and the count only. On a phone, a reader with a section to fill gets the request fold and About under the bar; every other reader keeps the page's order. Activity unavailable's link goes to the acting role's list, else Home.

R4 § Form system, "The activity form". The writer of a scale field chooses on the rung row (radios, six abreast, the descriptors under "What each rung means"), not a select. Moves are in sentence case everywhere. One note panel for every move that needs a note; danger only for a move that ends the activity.

5 · The reads

  • ListWaitingForYouQuery(caller): every activity a move of which, one that leads on, the caller may make now, leaving out the caller's own subject rows; oldest first by UpdatedOn; each row with its state label, UpdatedOn and whether it is overdue (more than 7 × 24 h). Returns the rows and their count. Home takes the first five and the count; the inbox takes all of them. One read, so the two cannot disagree (T297).
  • The paged decisions read (ListDecidedByYouQuery(caller, page, 20)): activities the caller moved last that are finished or have no move left, newest first by the caller's move; total for the pager. Home takes page 1's first five.
  • The way on's read: ListWaitingForYouQuery less the activity just moved: its first row and its count, read after the move commits.
  • The committee Home's line: for a holder of Assessor acting in another role, the same query's count, overdue count and first row. Nothing waiting: no line.
  • Each rung press is one round trip: the radio's value is bound on the server over the circuit, an explicit action per press, as every other control on the form. Only the fill and the "chosen" mark could follow :has(input:checked) with no script; the descriptor under the row is the server's render.
  • Waiting is one clock, UpdatedOn, counted as the nudge counts it: whole days, rounded down. Under one day: "Waiting less than a day" ("Less than a day" in the inbox's cell). Overdue: more than 7 whole days.
  • Step 3.30's ageing ages the Submit history row with UpdatedOn, so "asked you on 2026-09-22 08:06 SAST" and "since 2026-09-22 08:06 SAST" agree (E5).
