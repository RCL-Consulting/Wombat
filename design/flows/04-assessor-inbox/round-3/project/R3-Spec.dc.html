The writer of the rated level field chooses on the rung row (radios, as wide as its ladder, the descriptors under "What each rung means"); any other scale field, or a ladder that fails to load, keeps the select. Moves are in sentence case everywhere. One note panel for every move that needs a note; danger only for a move that ends the activity.

4 · Focus

  • (a) Decline or Return opens the panel: the focus moves to the note (#note-in).
  • (b) Keep the request / reflection / review: the focus goes back to the toggle, aria-expanded="false".
  • (c) Refused: #note-summary takes the focus; its link targets #note-in; the panel stays open with the note as typed.
  • (d) A completed move: the focus goes to #activity-result; the next Tab is Open the next (or Go to Home).
  • (e) Try again: the focus goes to the region that replaces the error.
  • (f) A page of Decided by you: the focus goes to its h2; Previous on page 1 is aria-disabled, not disabled, so the pressed button keeps its place.
  • The rung picker: arrow keys move and choose; Tab leaves the group; with none chosen, Tab lands on the first rung.

7 · The reads

  • ListWaitingForYouQuery(caller): every activity a move of which, one that leads on, the caller may make now, leaving out the caller's own subject rows; oldest first by UpdatedOn; each row with its state label, UpdatedOn and whether it is overdue (AssessorDueDays × 24 h). Rows and count. Home takes the first five and the count; the inbox all of them. One read, so the two cannot disagree (T297).
  • ListDecidedByYouQuery(caller, page, 20): activities the caller moved last that are finished or have no move left, newest first; the total for the pager. Home takes page 1's first five.
  • The way on's read: ListWaitingForYouQuery less the activity just moved: its first row and its count, read after the move commits.
  • The other-role line: for a holder of Assessor acting in another role, the same query's count, overdue count and first row. Nothing waiting, or a failed read: no line (the failure is logged).
  • Each rung press is one round trip: the radio's value is bound on the server over the circuit, an explicit action per press. Only the fill could follow :has(input:checked) with no script; the descriptor under the row is the server's render.
  • Waiting is one clock, UpdatedOn, counted as the nudge counts it: whole days, rounded down. Under one day: "Waiting less than a day" ("Less than a day" in the inbox's cell). Overdue from 7 whole days.
  • Step 3.30's ageing ages the Submit history row with UpdatedOn (E5).
