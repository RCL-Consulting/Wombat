Edge cases

  • 25 rows waiting: Home's card shows the first five and "and 20 more", its count "25 · 4 overdue"; the inbox shows all 25 in one page (25 a page), oldest first. C's Home is unchanged by volume.
  • Every row overdue: the card's stripe and count say "25 · 25 overdue"; every row keeps its state badge with Overdue beside it; no row loses its state. C's groups collapse to one, Overdue.
  • Two requests from one registrar on one EPA and date: the two links read the same to the eye ("Mini-CEX (Paediatrics) · PAED-001 · 2026-09-20, from Anele Dlamini"); the line under each says when it reached you ("Waiting 3 days, since 2026-09-27 08:10 SAST"), and each link's accessible name adds it ("…, filed 2026-09-27 08:10"), then "(1 of 2)" where the times match too (T280).
  • A two-role consultant with work in both views: each Home carries the other role's line (A) or card (B); C's switch counts only the other role's inbox. The committee's wording is flow 07's.
  • A request on a paused EPA: it stays in the list and on the card; the inbox's EPA column and About read "(no longer in use)"; completing it is not refused (D48); the status card reads "Its credit to PAED-012 waits while the EPA is paused."; Recent decisions shows it Completed, credit None.
  • An assessor who is also a registrar: their own requests never enter their inbox (the query leaves out the author's own work, T342); their acting role picks the Home; a Trainee's Home shows Needs you, an Assessor's shows Waiting for you; the other-role line (A) works both ways.
  • Long names: a 120-character EPA wraps under the link in muted text on the inbox, never on Home's card (which shows the code); a 30-character registrar name wraps on its own line.
  • 12 recent decisions: Home shows 5; the rest are one link away (A, B: the section; C: the page). None is out of reach.
  • No email on arrival (T320): no copy says or implies one; the count, the order and the Overdue badge are the notice. The 5-day nudge is not mentioned on the page.

Accessibility review (WCAG 2.1 AA)

  • Row actions named per row (2.4.4, 2.4.6): every row's link is the activity's name plus "from <registrar>" (a hidden ", " between the lines), unique on the page by filing time and "(1 of 2)" (T280). Open the next names its item. Home's rows meet it, which they do not today.
  • The count read as words (1.3.1): the badge is aria-hidden; a hidden ", 3 waiting, 1 overdue" follows the title (DashboardCard CountWords); the inbox's section headings the same.
  • Overdue not by colour alone (1.4.1): a word, "Overdue", in a badge beside the state; the stripe only repeats it.
  • Focus after each move (2.4.3, 3.2.2): the result region (#activity-result, tabindex="-1", role="status") takes it; the way on is inside it, so "Completed. 1 more waits for you" is read at once and Tab reaches Open the next. A refused note: the note summary. Keep the request: back to Decline. In C, the next page's result takes it on arrival.
  • Focus after the way on: Open the next loads the next activity; its h1 takes the focus (FocusOnNavigate). Back to Activity inbox: its h1.
  • The note panel named (4.1.2): section#note-panel aria-labelledby its h2; its toggle aria-expanded, aria-controls; the note labelled "Note for Sipho Ndlovu, required", described by its help and, when refused, by its message and the summary.
  • The rung picker by keyboard (2.1.1): one radio group, a fieldset named by its legend: Tab enters on the chosen rung (or the first), arrow keys move and choose, Space chooses; each radio is described by its own rung's descriptor, so "3b, radio, 4 of 6, [descriptor]". The focus ring is on the whole rung (:focus-within). Targets 50×56px at 390px.
  • Greyed moves (4.1.2): aria-disabled, in the tab order, described by their reason.
  • 390px (1.4.10): nothing scrolls sideways: the inbox stacks, the six rungs fit (6 × ≈50px + 5 × 4px in 326px), feedback fields are full width, the bar stacks, the history folds.
  • Times (T325): every time is "2026-09-30 10:27 SAST"; waits are days, with the SAST time under them.
