A · Home, an Assessor with three waiting, one overdue (Mohammed Patel)

  1. Waiting for you replaces "Waiting for your rating": it holds reviews and reflections to discuss too. Its rows are the inbox's rows, from the same read, in the same order (oldest first) and the same words: the first five, then "and 20 more" when there are more. NEW: WaitingList, the assessor's sibling of NeedsYouList.
  2. The count is a badge to the eye and words to a screen reader ("3 waiting, 1 overdue"). With nothing waiting there is no "0" badge: the card reads "Nothing is waiting for you."
  3. Recent decisions keeps five rows, newest first, each with its date. A decision: an activity you moved last that is now finished or has no move left.
  4. "All your decisions" opens the inbox's second section, Decided by you (question 1): every decision, paged.
  5. Each row: its link named "Type · EPA · date", a second line "from ", the state's badge, and Overdue beside it, never in its place; then how long it has waited. The row's link is its action (T280).
