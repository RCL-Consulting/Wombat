See what waits for me, and rate it, decline it or send it back

Three structures for the assessor's Home, the Activity inbox and their decisions, as wireframes (no colour or type choices), and the assessor's activity page drawn once in flow 03's design. Pick one structure; round 2 draws it at full fidelity.

A

Two pages, one list

Keep Home and the inbox. Home's card is the inbox's first five rows, from one read, in one order, in one set of words. Decisions become a second section of the inbox.

1 · Decisions
Recent decisions (5, dated) on Home; everything under "Decided by you" in the inbox, paged, no cut-off.
2 · Home vs inbox
Both. Home adds what the inbox lacks: the decisions and, later, figures of its own.
3 · Two roles
A line on the other view's Home, naming the oldest, with Open it (no switch) and Switch to Assessor.
4 · Overdue
Badge beside the state, oldest first, and the count "3 · 1 overdue".
Pages
Home, Activity inbox (two sections), the activity.

B

Home is the inbox

For the Assessor, one page: Home lists what waits, then what they decided. "Activity inbox" leaves the Assessor's menu. A two-role consultant's other Home carries the rows themselves.

1 · Decisions
"Decided by you" on Home, paged, no cut-off.
2 · Home vs inbox
Merged. Nothing can disagree because there is one list.
3 · Two roles
A card of the waiting rows on the committee Home; they open and are rated without switching.
4 · Overdue
As A.
Pages
Home (a list page), the activity.

C

A queue you work through

Home is a summary and a start ("Start with the oldest"). The activity page carries you on: after a move it opens the next item itself. Decisions get a page of their own.

1 · Decisions
My decisions, a third menu item, searchable, paged.
2 · Home vs inbox
Both, but Home shows no list, only figures and the oldest.
3 · Two roles
A count on the sidebar's switch, on every page.
4 · Overdue
A group heading the inbox, plus the badge and the count.
Pages
Home, Activity inbox (grouped), My decisions, the activity (with Next waiting).

  • Discard changes says why it is greyed. The Request reads names without emails. "Activity unavailable" offers Go to Activity inbox to an Assessor.

Recommendation: A

A makes T297 structural (one component, one read, one order) without moving the shell flow 01 settled; a two-role consultant keeps the same menu in each view and is told on the Home she opens to, with one press to the request. Decisions sit where the work is. B is the fewest pages and the shortest phone path, but it takes a nav item from the Assessor that the Coordinator's and committee's pages link to, and turns one role's Home into a list page while every other role's is a dashboard. C's straight-to-next saves a press but takes the rated record off the screen before its status card is read, and its count on the switch costs a read on every page. If you like B's two-role card, it fits A: A5's line can list the rows.

Your pick: A, B or C (or A with B's two-role card). Then round 2 draws it on the tokens, every screen and state, at 1280 and 390.
