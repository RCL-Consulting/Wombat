Notes

A2 — reading a College instrument

  1. 1 The standing notice is an info Alert with no role, there on every visit (T300). For an institution’s own type read by someone else it reads “Set by Kgosi Kgari Teaching Hospital.”; a Global one “Set by a global administrator.”
  2. 2 Form settings read as text, each field by its label with its key as a monospace detail (T324).
  3. 3 View opens under its row, as Edit does for a writer (question 4: in place). The scale reads by name, “CPSA Paediatric Entrustment Scale v11.1”, never its seed key (T271).
  4. 4 Version history sits on Details: number, date, publisher; a count of activities per version is DATA (question 3).
  5. 5 Workflow read in words (question 2): states, then a captioned table of moves — from, to, who, how much it checks, note. “Who” resolves field:assessor_user_id to the field’s label. The JSON is a fold beneath it, for the reader too.
  6. 6 Credit in words: what it counts for, what matches it, how much, and which field gives the level.
  7. 7 System-managed: the notice names its release and says the College cannot change it either (T334, PROPOSED words). No Discard, Publish, Add, Up, Down or Delete.
  8. The h1 is the type’s name (T190); tabs are h2-less, each panel’s first heading an h2. Headings: h1, h2, h3 (a section), never h3 straight under h1.
