Notes

C3 — a new type, stepped

  1. 1 New activity type walks Details → Form → Workflow → Credit → Review and publish NEW. Each step’s primary saves the draft and continues; a refusal stops it there.
  2. 2 The steps stay as the map for an existing type: any step is a link, so after version 1 it works as tabs with numbers. Each step its own address (/admin/activity-types/{id}/form …) PROPOSED: a new route.
  3. 3 Question 4: a panel beside the outline. The open row is striped; the panel’s heading names the field.
  4. 4 With no field open, the panel’s column holds the live preview; with one open, “Show the preview” swaps back. Cost: editing and previewing are never side by side.
  5. 5 A refusal names the step (“Step 2, Form:”) and the steps bar counts it.
  6. 6 At 390 the panel is the page while open; Done returns to the outline at the row.
