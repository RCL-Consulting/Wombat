Notes

B1 — the list, grouped by whose it is

  1. 1 Whose it is becomes the structure: an h2 per owner (flow 16’s h2 per speciality), each a captioned table without a Set by column; the scope stays a column. Hers first, then her College’s, then the Demo College’s PROPOSED.
  2. 2 The College admin sees his College first; the system-managed rows sit in it, View, with their release named.
  3. 3 Search with Show, as A. No match, empty, loading, load error and the Administrator’s list are drawn on A1 and are the same here (the Administrator’s groups: each College, then each institution).
  4. Cost: 3 tables to scan instead of one sortable list; gain: Edit and View fall out of the grouping, read at a glance.
