Accessibility review (WCAG 2.1 AA)

  • One h1 per page: the type’s name, “Edit <name>” or “New activity type”; h2 for each panel’s parts or each section; h3 for a form section in the outline. No h3 straight under the h1 (today’s h1 → h3 goes).
  • Every table captioned: the list, each owner group (B), Moves in words, Credit rules in words, Published versions.
  • Selected part exposed: A role=tablist/tab with aria-selected and arrow keys; B the contents list is plain links (focus by script); C aria-current="step".
  • Row actions named for their row: “Edit field Audience”, “Delete section Teaching session”, “Move field EPA up”, “View Mini-CEX (Paediatrics)”.
  • Focus: after Save draft, the result (or the refusal summary); after Done, the row’s Edit; after a ConfirmDialog, its result; after Delete, the next row’s Edit (or Add field); after Show, the count line.
  • Refusals tied to their inputs with aria-describedby, refusal first; invalid marked by border and stripe, not colour alone; a refused move’s cell striped and worded.
  • Targets: 24px+ at 1280 (btn-sm 28px); 44px under 641px for every control drawn at 390.
  • Publish when off is aria-disabled (in the tab order, its reason read); A.7.14’s exemption for a disabled control still holds for aria-disabled, and white on Publish is 5.88:1 when on.
  • The C diagram is aria-hidden: the table carries it. The preview is the real renderer, its own labels.
  • Contrast unchanged: every pair is an existing token pair (README § Contrast); the tab underline secondary on surface 4.86:1; the danger stripe 5.95:1.
