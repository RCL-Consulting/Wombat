Notes

A3 — a new type and its form

  1. 1 A new type opens on Details PROPOSED: Key and Name are the first things to write. Scope is a read-out when it has one choice; a picker otherwise.
  2. 2 Scope offers exactly the writable scopes, starting in the first (T300, D52); Sub-speciality’s targets by their College path.
  3. 3 The first save moves to /admin/activity-types/{id} and the result survives the move (flow 15’s one-shot token), taking the focus. Publish is offered now, its reason gone.
  4. 4 Rows read label, then type by its label and key in monospace, then Required: no “epa_id · Epa”. Actions sit right, clear of the label at 1280 (the outline’s column is 380px+).
  5. 5 Edit opens under the row pressed (question 4), the row and editor sharing a stripe. Done keeps the change in the draft; nothing is saved until Save draft.
  6. 6 A refusal shows in the summary (the shipped sentence, linked to the field) and at each field it names (PROPOSED words, a field-level mapping DATA); the tab carries a count, so a refusal on another tab is never silent.
  7. 7 Names a lists roles by label; Assessor the default. Help verbatim.
  8. 8 Delete is an outline button that asks nothing: it changes nothing until Save draft (flow 16’s Remove). A form setting pointing at a deleted field stays shown, marked “not in the form”, so it can be cleared.
  9. 9 At 390 the preview is one press away, not 4,390px down (T323). The tabs fit one row (four short labels, 87px each).
