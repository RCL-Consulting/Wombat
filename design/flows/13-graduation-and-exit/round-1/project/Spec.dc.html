PROPOSED (yours to decide)

  • My authorisations admits the trainee record (TraineeOrFormerTrainee) and its Download works; the EPA page's “Open My authorisations” shows to her (T311, Q3). MSF reports and My committee reviews stay role-only.
  • The owner table: for a former trainee, Export portfolio, My activities and My authorisations sit under My progress (trail Home › My progress › the page); for staff, the export sits under Trainees (Institutional admin) or Programme trainees (Coordinator, Committee member, both speciality admins), its trail Home › the list › the registrar › Export portfolio.
  • S1-B: three new personal links. S1-C: a header action on the no-role Home. S2-C: a new item and route, Portfolio exports.
  • The staff export names the trainee: a name query gated by MayReadAsync, answering nothing for an id the caller may not export (T314). Refused before any form: V1 today's sentence, V2 Page not found, V3 its own refusal page.
  • Export's dates default to the programme start and today, or the last day, on the South African calendar (T325); the result names the file.
  • Verify: any letter case and any whitespace accepted; a value that is not 64 hex digits refused under the field; several exports answer with the first, the count and the last (ORDER BY ExportedOn, Id); the records kept for the life of the trainee record (the 90-day job goes); help with the commands; the time in SAST (Q6).
  • S5: V1 checks the day when Mark complete… or Deactivate… is pressed, before the dialog; V2 adds the choice; V3 a page of its own. The refusal “The last day cannot be after today (2026-10-26).” under the field, the typed day kept and focused.
  • The profile's h1 is the person; Back to trainees goes (the trail is the way back); an unknown id is Page not found drawn in place; a load error has Try again; a failed end says nothing changed and keeps the day.
  • Completed and closed: the latest end first, View named for the row, 20 a page.
  • T312: an ended record keeps the EPA list and targets it ended with; a paused EPA stays, marked; a later item does not appear.

Edge cases

  • No STAR (du Plessis): A and C say so with no link; B keeps My authorisations in the menu, which opens on its empty card.
  • Later holds Assessor: the Assessor's Home; A adds an info line, C a record card, B keeps the four personal links.
  • Neither a role nor a record: flow 01's “No role assigned”, unchanged in all three.
  • A hash with spaces or a line break: stripped before the check; V3's box shows the paste as it came.
  • A 12-digit prefix: its own refusal, not “No matching export found”.
  • Staff who also oversee at another institution: the export's scope is the registrar's institution; the gated query answers per id, so an id elsewhere refuses the same way.
  • A profile moved before the end: the export header and the closed list read the curriculum the programme ended on (T312).
  • A last day in a period's last month: that period keeps its target (today's rule, in the help).
  • The end on the programme's first day: allowed; My progress lists one period, no target.
  • From after To: refused under From, both dates kept.
  • Two people with one name in the closed list: the email under the name, and in View's accessible name (flow 11's rule).
  • A 60-character name: the h1 wraps; the account row cuts at 28 characters; the dialog's heading wraps.

Accessibility (WCAG 2.1 AA)

  • One h1 a page; Verify's answer, the profile's sections and the export's cards are h2 (T280).
  • Every table captioned without repeating its heading; View, Export… and Download named for their row.
  • Focus: the export's result, the verify answer after a press (not on arrival from a link), the ended record's result after a confirm; a refusal's field; Cancel returns focus to the trigger.
  • Every refusal tied to its field by aria-describedby, the field marked twice (border and stripe).
  • Targets 24px at 1280, 44px at 390 (row actions, Try again, the record links, the dialog's buttons).
  • Verify is a GET form and plain HTML: it works with no script.
