DESIGN.md text each change needs

DESIGN.md sectionThe text it changes
§ Dashboard page (no role)A record holder's Home is one emphasised card, “Your training record”, led by the end and its day (“You completed your programme on 2026-10-26.” / “Your programme ended on 2026-10-24.”), that the record is kept read-only, then the record's pages, each a link with a line on what it holds; My authorisations only for a holder of a STAR, plain text otherwise; no subtitle, no header action. A former trainee holding a staff role keeps that role's Home, with no record line (Q25).
§ The NavMenu's personal linksA trainee record without the Trainee role adds Export portfolio between My progress and My data rights (Q2); My authorisations and My activities are pages under My progress.
§ Detail page and the title ruleA trainee's profile is titled “‹name›'s programme” (h1, tab, crumb; M3), the user page keeps the name; the staff export is titled “Export ‹name›'s portfolio”; an ended record shows the outcome first.
§ Form pageExport's dates open on the programme start and today, or the last day, on the South African calendar; both required; the result above the form, focused, naming the file; From after To refused under From.
§ Button systemThe end's two triggers are outline buttons in their own section, apart from Save profile; Mark complete's confirm is primary, Deactivate's is filled danger (Q24); both dialogs say what cannot be undone.
§ Table systemCompleted and closed: Name as the row's header, View (not Edit), the latest end first, a caption that does not repeat the heading, StackWide, a pager past 20.
§ Page-level patternsThe Unavailable page for the export (“Portfolio unavailable”) and the profile (“Profile unavailable”): one body for every cause, no form, one way on. Flow 12's “User unavailable” keeps its own h1 “User”; these use the unavailable words as the h1, as Q19 and S12 decide.
§ Account / auth page's static pagesVerify's answer is an h2 section, focused after a press and left alone on arrival from a link; the field takes any case and whitespace.
