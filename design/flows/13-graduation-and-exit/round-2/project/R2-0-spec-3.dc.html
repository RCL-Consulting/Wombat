Edge cases

  • No STAR (du Plessis): Home's row plain text (R2-H02); no My authorisations on the record line (R2-R02); its page, reached by address, the empty card (R2-R09).
  • A staff role later: her staff Home, no record line; personal links keep My progress and Export portfolio (R2-H03).
  • Neither role nor record: “No role assigned”, menu Home and My data rights (R2-H04).
  • The pasted hash: spaces and the line break stripped by the query (R2-V09).
  • The 12-digit prefix: its own refusal (R2-V06).
  • Another institution: the export's header and scope from the registrar's institution (R2-E17); another institution's profile is Profile unavailable (R2-P13).
  • A moved profile: the ended record reads the curriculum it ended on (R2-P18, after T312).
  • A period's last month: the dialog says that semester keeps its target (R2-P17).
  • The first day: allowed, the dialog names it (R2-P16).
  • From after To: refused under From (R2-E11).
  • Two people with one name: the address under each name and in View's name (R2-C05).
  • A 60-character name: the top bar cuts at 28, the menu's foot wraps (R2-H07); the h1, trail and actions wrap (R2-E18, R2-P19).

Accessibility (WCAG 2.1 AA)

  • One h1 a page frame, h2 a section, h3 below; Verify's answer and the profile's sections are h2.
  • Every table captioned, scope on its headers, DataTable's roles kept when stacked; every row action named for its row.
  • Focus: drawn with .anno-focus and named on each board's chip: the result after Export and after a confirm; the answer after Verify, not from a link; a refusal's field; each dialog opens on Cancel, Cancel and Escape return to the trigger; Try again to the reloaded page's h1.
  • Help outside the label, named by aria-describedby; an invalid field marked twice (border and stripe) and named by its message; a standing notice an Alert with no role; status only for what arrives with a result.
  • Verify is a GET form: it works with no script.

Sideways scroll, measured

Rendered each board's markup with bundle.css v33 in Chromium (the canvas's fonts fall back to Segoe UI, Georgia or the system's, so heights are near, not exact): every 1280 board at 1280 and again at 1100 with its width freed, every 768 board at 768, every 390 board at 390. No page scrolls sideways at any of the four widths: the document's scroll width equals the viewport on all 240 renders. The only boxes past the right edge are the visually hidden column headers of tables stacked by StackWide or Stack (clipped, never painted). The 64-digit hashes and the user ids wrap inside their cells (.hash-value, .verify-details' minmax(0, 1fr)); a 60-character name wraps in the h1 and the dialog title.
