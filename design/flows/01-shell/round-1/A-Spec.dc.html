b

Several roles: Dr Zulu

  • She acts in one role at a time. The sidebar's head says so on every page: "Acting as · Committee member", then "Switch to Assessor".
  • Her navigation is that role's only: 4 links as Committee member, 3 as Assessor (today 9, merged).
  • Three or more roles: the head shows "Change role" and opens a short list of the others (a native disclosure, no script).
  • A link into the other role's page (an emailed rating request) switches her to that role and says so in the head and in a status line under the page header. A page both roles admit keeps the current role.
d

Switching

  • Lives in the shell (sidebar head), so it is on every page, not on Home only.
  • Changes the navigation and the landing: the switch opens the new role's Home, with "You are now acting as Assessor." as an info ActionResult; focus moves to it.
  • Remembered for the session; forgotten at sign-out (T317). Next sign-in lands by the brief's precedence: Committee member before Assessor.
  • Authorisation does not change: a page admits the union of her roles. The acting role only chooses what the frame shows.
c

Top bar

  • Full width, 56 px, sticky. Left: the mark and Wombat over the sidebar column (the brand row leaves the sidebar, which buys the Administrator's list its height).
  • Right: the person's name, a link to My Account, at least 32 px tall; then one Sign out button. Nothing else.
  • A long name truncates at 28 characters with an ellipsis; the whole name is the link's accessible name and title. An account with no name shows its email, truncated the same way.
  • Signed out: the mark and a Sign in button.
fg

States, and the phone

  • Access denied, not found and the error page render in the content column of the one shell; signed out, in the static shell (top bar only). Never a second layout.
  • The in-app error bar sits at the foot of the content column, above nothing else; the reconnect dialog is modal over the whole shell. See the States page.
  • Below 641 px the sidebar folds behind a CSS-only Menu toggle in the top bar. Folded, the bar still names the acting role. Open, it lists the role head, the links, then the name and Sign out.

Edge cases

  • 17 links on an 800 px screen: fits at 28 px rows under a 56 px bar (about 700 px of list). A browser window loses 80–120 px to its own chrome, so the sidebar scrolls on its own: a focusable scroll region that shows the focus ring (T328), with the active item scrolled into view.
  • A role label that wraps ("Sub-speciality admin", "Switch to Sub-speciality admin"): the head grows to two lines; nothing truncates a role.
  • A long name or email: truncated in the bar, whole in the phone menu, where it wraps.
  • The active item: exactly one per page (T331). A detail page lights its owning list; a page no list owns lights Home.

DESIGN.md rules and tests it changes

  • Keeps: the 250 px sidebar, the 641 px breakpoint, the 16 px phone gutter.
  • Changes: the top row spans the full width and carries the brand; the brand row leaves the sidebar. The nav's order, grouping and labels (Main page). My Account and Logout leave the nav.
  • New rules: flat up to 8, grouped above; no link to an unbuilt page; one acting role, shown in the shell.
  • Tests: nav snapshot per role (new lists); unique-label and admits-role tests unchanged, rerun on the new lists; new: one active item per route per role; switching changes the nav; the remembered role clears at sign-out.
