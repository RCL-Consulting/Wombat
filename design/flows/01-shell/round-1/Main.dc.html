Three ways to hold the navigation

Greyscale boxes and real labels; no colour or type choices yet. Each variation has its own page on this canvas: the Administrator's shell, Dr Zulu before and after changing role, the Trainee's phone folded and open, and a sheet with every role's navigation and the click counts. Outcomes and failure states are shared by all three and have their own page. Pick one (or name the parts to mix) before round 2.

Variation A

Grouped sidebar, one role at a time

  • The sidebar holds the acting role's links: a flat list up to 8, grouped under headings above 8.
  • The acting role and the switch head the sidebar, on every page.
  • Switching changes the navigation and the landing.
Variation B

Top-bar sections, all your roles at once

  • The top bar lists pages (up to 5 besides Home) or sections; a section's pages sit in a second row.
  • No role mode: you see everything you may do, and each page names the role it serves.
  • Home holds each role's work; you choose which role leads it.
Variation C

Home is the map

  • No navigation bar. Home lists the acting role's waiting work, then every page it may open, grouped.
  • Every other page carries breadcrumbs and its group's other pages.
  • The role switch sits in the top bar and changes Home.
