The reconnect dialog: one message per state, and its button says what the text says

A native modal dialog over the whole shell, centred, 360 px wide (full width less 16 px gutters on a phone). Its heading takes focus when it opens; the button, where there is one, is the only control. Motion: the progress bar only, and it stops under reduced motion.
