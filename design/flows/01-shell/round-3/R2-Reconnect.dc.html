Reconnect dialog: one message per state; the button says what the text says

A native <dialog> over the shell on the scrim token. 400 px in the product (372 here); 358 px at 390, 16 px each side. The heading takes focus when it opens. The grey behind this sheet is canvas-only.

1 · Rejoining

Reconnecting

The connection to Wombat dropped. Reconnecting now.

No button: nothing to do yet.

2 · Retrying, waiting

Could not reconnect

Trying again in 5 seconds.

No button. The count runs down each second.

3 · Retrying, attempt started

Could not reconnect

Trying again now.

The same line, replaced when the attempt starts.

4 · Failed

Connection lost

Wombat cannot be reached. Try again when your connection is back. If the page cannot be restored, it reloads, and anything not yet saved on it is lost.

5 · Paused

Page paused

This page is paused. Resume to carry on.

6 · Resume failed

Could not resume

Wombat cannot be reached. Try again when your connection is back. If the page cannot be restored, it reloads, and anything not yet saved on it is lost.

7 · Rejected (the server no longer holds the page)

Reloading

Reloading the page…

No button: the reload is already under way.

Rules

Each state replaces the last; two never show at once.

No "Try now": the retry runs on its own, and its line changes when it starts.

Failed and Resume failed share one sentence and one button, Try again (T330).

Under prefers-reduced-motion the spinner and the bar stop, the dialog does not slide or fade, and transitions go to 0; the words carry the state.

Icons, Lucide: loader-circle, clock, circle-alert, pause, refresh-cw.
