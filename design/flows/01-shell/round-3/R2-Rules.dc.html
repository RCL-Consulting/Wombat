Roles, switching and the current item

1 · Access and the acting role

  1. Access is the union of the roles a person holds, and never changes. The acting role chooses only what the frame shows: the sidebar, the landing and the head. A page opens if any held role admits it.
  2. In-app navigation never switches role. A link inside Wombat to a page outside the acting role's navigation opens it and keeps the role; nothing is lit (see the owner table).
  3. Only a link that names a role switches, and only to a role the person holds: /dashboard/switch/{role}?returnUrl=<local path>. Emails use it. A role the person does not hold is ignored: the role stays and the return address opens as usual. A return address that is not local is dropped, and the switch lands on that role's Home.
  4. The sidebar's "Switch to …" is the same link without a return address, so it lands on the new role's Home.
  5. "Switch back" returns to the same page: /dashboard/switch/CommitteeMember?returnUrl=/activities/{id}. The page stays (access has not changed); the sidebar and the head change.
  6. After any switch the result reads "You are now acting as Assessor." in an info ActionResult under the page header, and focus moves to it.

2 · What is remembered

  • The acting role is remembered per account, across sign-ins: Thandi Zulu signs in next week acting as whatever she last chose.
  • It is stored with the account, not in the browser. The next person to sign in on the same browser lands by the normal precedence (Administrator, College admin, Institutional admin, Speciality admin, Sub-speciality admin, Committee member, Coordinator, Assessor, Trainee, Pending trainee). T317 holds: nothing of one person's choice outlives their sign-out on that browser.

3 · The current item: owner table

A list lights itself with aria-current="page". A page under a list lights that list, its owner for the acting role, with aria-current="true". At most one item is lit. Where the acting role's navigation holds no owner, nothing is lit: "lights Home" is retired. Breadcrumbs follow the owning list.
