The Administrator's list at a 1280 × 700 viewport

  • The brand cell (56 px) and the role head stay fixed; only the list below them scrolls. At 700 px the list needs about 680 px and has about 600, so it scrolls by about 90 px.
  • Rows are 28 px with 4 px between them. The 2 px ring sits 2 px outside the row, so it never meets the next row.
  • The list pads 4 px at the top and 8 px at the sides and foot, so the first and last rings are not clipped by the scroll edge.
  • The list takes no tabindex. Tab moves through the links, and the browser scrolls each focused link into view.
  • The current item is scrolled into view on load (scroll-margin-block: 8px).
  • Hover and the scrollbar (drawn here as a thumb) are the browser's; the thumb is canvas-only.
