2 · DESIGN.md rules, ready to paste

R1 § Dashboard page (the Trainee's Home)

The Trainee's Home holds four cards, in this order: Your targets (spanning two, the emphasis stripe), Needs you, Recent decisions (spanning two) and My authorisations. - Your targets: "Training year N — it sets the minimum level each encounter is judged against."; the two figures in My progress's words ("1 of 10" over "EPAs met this semester", "0 of 5" over "EPAs met in 2026"); then "Furthest short", the five EPAs furthest short, largest shortfall first, then by code. Each row is its own link to its EPA page, named "Code — Title", with its figure and date under the name, never beside it, and a bar that never stands without the words. Footer: Open My progress. - Needs you: flow 03's NeedsYouList, unchanged; its "Open My activities" is Home's one way to My activities. - Recent decisions: the registrar's own requests that someone else last moved and that are finished (a terminal state of the pinned workflow) or have no move left: Completed, Discussed, Signed off, Declined, Accepted, Reviewed, Approved, Verified, Rejected, and any other terminal state. Newest decision first, five rows, no footer. Each row: ActivityLink ("Type · EPA · date", "to <assessor>" under it; the assessor joins the name when two rows would share it), the decision's badge (BadgeFor.ActivityState: badge-completed for a finished state, badge-declined for Declined and Rejected) and its day, then one line by kind. The count line follows one rule with the completed card's sentence: the activity's own window, named when it is not the current one ("PAED-001, Semester 1, 2026: 3 of 3, met."), the current one as "PAED-001: 3 of 3 this semester, met."; "Credits nothing." wherever it credited nothing; "File it again, to someone else" for Declined alone. MSF records are not listed. Flow 04's classes (.decided-list, .decided-row, .decided-meta, .decided-date) plus .decided-note. - My authorisations: the standing summary in the panel's words, then each STAR below its level or expiring within 30 days, a link to its EPA page; footer Open My authorisations. The card's title is the page's name. A card is never one link around other links; a card's rows are its links. No MSF on Home. No Upcoming deadlines: a date is shown on the row it belongs to.

R2 § Page-level patterns

A record page under a personal link: the EPA page, /portfolio/progress/{EpaId:int}, owned by My progress for every role, and for a graduate who holds none (one rule in NavOwners.OwnerFor for a page under a personal link; ActiveNavItemTests reads the page's policy, TraineeOrFormerTrainee). It lights My progress with aria-current="true"; the trail is Home › My progress › <code>. Addressed by the EPA's id, never its code (a code is unique only in its namespace). Its h1 is "<code> — <title>", with "(no longer in use)" after it for a paused EPA; the tab is the h1's words, mark included ("PAED-012 — Communicating with and counselling patients, caregivers and healthcare teams (no longer in use) · Wombat"); the crumb stays the code ("PAED-012"). The h1 takes the focus on arrival (FocusOnNavigate). "Open My authorisations" shows only to a Trainee (My authorisations is Roles = "Trainee"); a graduate reads the STAR in the Entrustment section. An unknown or out-of-scope id is "Page not found".

R3 The progress figures

A figure is a count against a target for a named window: "1 of 3 this semester", "0 of 1 in 2026", "1 of 10 EPAs met this semester". The decision cadence names its own frame ("Decided each semester", "Decided once a year", "Decided as opportunity allows", or no label where the item has none). A level names its training year, once per EPA: "Training year 4: level 5, the minimum each encounter is judged against and your STAR's target." A waived window shows its count and when targets start ("No target this semester · 2 recorded · targets start with semester 1, 2027"), never a fraction or a bar. Never "n / m", a percentage or a lifetime total; never the bare word "year" beside a training year and an academic year.

R4 § Entrustment standing, and the trajectory

EntrustmentStandingPanel and TrajectoryChart are changed once, for My progress and the committee's review page. - The panel stacks below 641px (DataTable's Stack: each cell but the EPA labelled by its column); its EPA and rating links are 44px blocks at phone width; its dates are ISO. Its EPA name links to the trainee's EPA page when the reader is the trainee, and to that EPA's chart on the committee page (a parameter). Everything else is the built panel, whole. - The chart is drawn twice in the markup, at 900px and at 326px, never scaled. The card, not the viewport, chooses: a container query on .trajectory-figure (container-type: inline-size) shows the 900 drawing where the wrapper holds 900px and the 326 drawing below that, with no script. The EPA page's card holds 900px only from a viewport of about 1254px (sidebar 250, the article's gutters, the card's padding), so at 1100 it draws 326. .trajectory-figure is also a named, focusable scroll region (role="region", aria-label "Rating chart for PAED-001", tabindex="0", as .table-container): the floor for a card narrower than 326px, such as the committee page's nested card (274px inside), where the 326 drawing scrolls sideways in its region and nothing else does. - One academic year on a real time axis with month ticks (every other month labelled in the 326 drawing); semester 2 banded; the area below the training year's minimum shaded and edged by a stepped dashed line in warning-color that steps where the training year changed; "Minimum 5" in text-color; the exit level a 2.5px primary-color line; a rating on another scale a hollow point in its own lane. Points closer than a dot are set a dot apart, in date order; the table gives each date. Rungs and months 13px (12px in the 326 drawing), nothing under 11px. Its table is always visible: Encounter, Rating, Against the minimum then, Activity (linked, named per row), Assessor, oldest first. The SVG is named for what it adds ("Chart of the 3 ratings in the table below."). The rule that a lone chart scales with its card (near line 2777) is withdrawn.
