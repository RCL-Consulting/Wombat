Flow 05 · Round 1 · Three structures for the registrar's Home and My progress

Wireframes only: no colour or type is chosen yet. The data is Anele Dlamini's (training year 3) and Lerato Molefe's (training year 4), Semester 2, 2026. A title in [brackets] is one the brief does not give.
V1 · EPA PAGES

An index, and a page per EPA

My progress is one table of every EPA (its count for its window, how often the committee decides it, its STAR against the training year's target, the exit level), grouped Each semester / Once a year. Each EPA name opens its own page: count this window and last, the minimum now, its STAR, its trajectory and the activities that counted.
Home: Your targets (training year, both figures, the five furthest short with their dates), Needs you, Your STARs. Recent activities and Upcoming deadlines go.
V2 · ONE PAGE

Every EPA a row that opens in place

My progress is one list ordered by what is due: unmet this semester, unmet this academic year, met, no longer in use. Each row is a native disclosure; open, it holds the same four things as V1's page. The committee's Annexure A table sits folded at the foot.
Home: Due this semester, Needs you, Coming up (real dates: window end, STAR expiry, a review scheduled for her), Credited lately (what each recent activity counted).
V3 · WEEKLY HOME

Home is the week; My progress in two parts

Home becomes the weekly page: one dated Due list merged from every source. My progress splits into Observations (per-window counts, evidence in a fold) and Entrustment (the committee's Annexure A table, the exit rule, then the trajectories). An EPA is in two places, linked both ways.
Closest to today's build; EntrustmentStandingPanel moves whole. My progress stays under the rule.
