2 · DESIGN.md rules, ready to paste

R1 § Dashboard page (the Trainee's Home)

The Trainee's Home holds four cards, in this order: Your targets (spanning two, the emphasis stripe), Needs you, Recent decisions (spanning two) and My authorisations. - Your targets: "Training year N — it sets the minimum level each encounter is judged against."; the two figures in My progress's words ("1 of 10" over "EPAs met this semester", "0 of 5" over "EPAs met in 2026"); then "Furthest short", the five EPAs furthest short, largest shortfall first, then by code. Each row is its own link to its EPA page, named "Code — Title", with its figure and date under the name, never beside it, and a bar that never stands without the words. Footer: Open My progress. - Needs you: flow 03's NeedsYouList, unchanged; its "Open My activities" is Home's one way to My activities. - Recent decisions: the registrar's own requests that someone else last moved and that are finished or have no move left (Completed, Discussed, Signed off, Declined), newest decision first, five rows, no footer. Each row: ActivityLink ("Type · EPA · date", "to <assessor>" under it; the assessor joins the name when two rows would share it), the decision's badge and its day, then one line by kind. MSF records are not listed. Flow 04's classes (.decided-list, .decided-row, .decided-meta, .decided-date) plus .decided-note. - My authorisations: the standing summary in the panel's words, then each STAR below its level or expiring within 30 days, a link to its EPA page; footer Open My authorisations. The card's title is the page's name. A card is never one link around other links; a card's rows are its links. No MSF on Home. No Upcoming deadlines: a date is shown on the row it belongs to.

R2 § Page-level patterns

A record page under a personal link: the EPA page, /portfolio/progress/{EpaId:int}, owned by My progress (it lights My progress with aria-current="true"; the trail is Home › My progress › <code>). Addressed by the EPA's id, never its code (a code is unique only in its namespace). Its h1 is "<code> — <title>", the tab "<code> — <title> · Wombat", and the h1 takes the focus on arrival (FocusOnNavigate). An unknown or out-of-scope id is "Page not found".

R3 The progress figures

A figure is a count against a target for a named window: "1 of 3 this semester", "0 of 1 in 2026", "1 of 10 EPAs met this semester". The decision cadence names its own frame ("Decided each semester", "Decided once a year", "Decided as opportunity allows", or no label where the item has none). A level names its training year, once per EPA: "Training year 4: level 5, the minimum each encounter is judged against and your STAR's target." A waived window shows its count and when targets start ("2 observed in Semester 2, 2026"; "Targets begin with Semester 1, 2027"), never a fraction or a bar. Never "n / m", a percentage or a lifetime total; never the bare word "year" beside a training year and an academic year.

R4 § Entrustment standing, and the trajectory

EntrustmentStandingPanel and TrajectoryChart are changed once, for My progress and the committee's review page. - The panel stacks below 641px (DataTable's Stack: each cell but the EPA labelled by its column); its EPA and rating links are 44px blocks at phone width; its dates are ISO. Its EPA name links to the trainee's EPA page when the reader is the trainee, and to that EPA's chart on the committee page (a parameter). - The chart is drawn at its own size at each width (900px wide; 326px below 641px, its card padded 16px), never scaled. One academic year on a real time axis with month ticks (every other month labelled at 326px); semester 2 banded; the area below the training year's minimum shaded and edged by a stepped dashed line in warning-color that steps where the training year changed; the exit level a 2.5px primary-color line; a rating on another scale a hollow point in its own lane. Points closer than a dot are set a dot apart, in date order; the table gives each date. Rungs and months 13px (12px narrow), nothing under 11px. Its table is always visible: Encounter, Rating, Against the minimum then, Activity (linked, named per row), Assessor, oldest first. The SVG is named for what it adds ("Chart of the 3 ratings in the table below."). The rule that a lone chart scales with its card (near line 2777) is withdrawn.

R5 My progress once the programme has ended (flow 13's)

Holds: for a former trainee My progress stays under the rule with every period read-only and no "this period" figures; the EPA page shows that EPA's periods the same way, read-only, with no "n more by" line.

5 · Focus

  • The EPA page's h1 takes the focus on arrival, by FocusOnNavigate: no script, no ?epa query, no scroll script. "Open My progress", a Home row, an index row, a STAR row and My activities' Credit link all land there.
  • Try again keeps the focus while it runs (aria-disabled, "Trying again…"); the answer takes it: This period's heading on My progress, the EPA's h1 on its page, Home's ActionResult on Home, or the error sentence again if it fails again. As My activities does (StatePanel, FocusAfterRetry).
  • A section's Try again (the standing) answers into the panel's heading.
  • On Home nothing moves the focus on load; the h1 has it after navigation, as flow 01 built.
