2 · What DESIGN.md changes

SectionChange
§ Form systemAn activity form is a stack of sections, each a .detail-card one deep on the page (never a card in a card). A section someone else fills in is .form-section--locked: a dashed input-border frame on the page's ground, no inputs, the owner's name with lock. Filled, it is a plain card of read-outs with "Filled in by {name}, {date}". Required fields carry a visible * and aria-required; help sits under the control. The action bar is .form-actions--moves: the move that moves it on is the one filled button, first; Save draft outlined; Cancel last and quiet, behind ConfirmDialog. A named nominee puts the name on the button ("Submit to Fatima Khumalo") only when the type has exactly one nominee field and it is filled; otherwise the move's own label. A move its mover cannot make is greyed with aria-disabled and a visible reason starting with its name. Below 641px every control is 44px and the bar stacks, primary on top.
§ Alerts, validation, empty statesA refusal is the validation summary (focus, links) plus the message beside each field; a changed value drops both at once and the form's hints (.field-warning) return. A move's outcome is the result region (.action-result, focus). A copy made from another record says so in an info notice. Load failures follow Home's pattern with Try again; no page prints an exception.
§ Page-level patternsA record page with a workflow opens with the status card (who has it now, what next, the one action), replacing the summary card and the state under the h1. Then .details-grid: About (the people, EPA, dates, credit) and the sections. The history closes the page: a table oldest first, each note on its own full-width line, times in SAST; below 641px it is stacked blocks behind a native <details> ("All 3 moves"). The h1 is type · EPA · date; the people are in the subtitle; the tab is the h1 + " · Wombat"; the last crumb is the h1. A list of work opens with Needs you when the reader has any.

5 · Open points for your review

  • E2 and T280 collide on a re-filing. A request filed again after a decline has the same type, EPA and date as the declined one, so the two share an h1, tab and crumb. Links in lists stay distinct (the assessor is a second line inside the link), but the headings do not. Options: add the assessor to the h1 only when a sibling shares it, or always.
  • A name with no EPA or date yet. Drawn as "Mini-CEX (Paediatrics) · no EPA yet · no date yet" (a draft saved empty) and "DOPS (Paediatrics) · PAED-002 · no date" (cancelled).
  • The portfolio review's reviewer. Step 3.22 names Dr Patel as reviewer, but correction 2 says any reviewer may take it. Drawn as correction 2 says, with no reviewer field shown; if the form keeps a reviewer field, its label should say it is a request, not an assignment.
  • The trainee's nav shows only the links this flow touches (Home, Log an activity, My activities, then My progress and My data rights); the rest is flow 01's, unchanged.
  • Home's card is titled "Needs you", not "Activity inbox", because the page it used to open is gone for a registrar; its footer opens My activities.
  • Discard changes stays, disabled until something is typed (step 3.2).
