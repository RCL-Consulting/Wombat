2 · Rules

  • Move labels (E4, C3). A button, a history row and a result use the move's own label from the workflow: Submit, Log, Cancel, Complete, Decline, Return, Record Discussion, Sign Off. The button adds " to <name>" only when the move leads to a state whose next move belongs to the person a filled user field names (the Mini-CEX's Assessor, the reflection's Supervisor or mentor, the review's Reviewer). A user field that receives nothing (the log's Supervising consultant) adds nothing: the log's button stays "Log". An empty, or no longer offered, nominee gives the plain label ("Submit").
  • Cancel's label (C11). "Cancel request…" while another person holds it; "Cancel this draft…" on a draft, including a returned one. Always last and quiet, always behind ConfirmDialog.
  • File it again (E1, E5). Only on a Declined request, and only to its registrar. It opens /activities/new?from={id} with the type, EPA, date and request copied and the nominee empty; nothing is saved until she saves or submits.
  • Names (E2, E7, E9). The h1, tab title and last crumb are type · EPA · date. When a re-filing shares type · EPA · date with another of the registrar's activities, all three add " · <nominee>" ("Mini-CEX (Paediatrics) · PAED-002 · 2026-09-09 · Sarah Botha"), and so does each one's link in lists. A draft saved empty: "Mini-CEX (Paediatrics) · no EPA yet · no date yet".
  • A locked section (C14). A head row on header-bg (lock, title, owner) over a body on the page's ground, inside the dashed frame. Before: "<name> fills this in" and, where useful, "It holds: …". On a closed record: "<name> was to fill this in." (or "The assessor was to fill this in." when none was named). Filled: a plain card with "Filled in by <name>, <date>".
  • A stored nominee no longer offered (C4). The picker never lists a locked assessor (A.6.6). A draft that already names one shows "<name> (not on the current list)" and is refused at submit with the field message.
  • Drafts are not private (C2). Programme staff in scope can list a draft, and a named assessor can open it by its address. The pages say only "It is in nobody's inbox until you submit it."
  • Focus (C8, C10). Summary links point at the input (#<field>-in), the note panel's at #note-in. Decline is a toggle (aria-expanded, aria-controls="note-panel"): opening moves the focus into the note; Keep the request closes the panel and returns the focus to Decline.
  • Forms (E6, C6). Fields keep the College's seed order and labels. Mini-CEX: EPA; Assessor; Date observed; Clinical setting; Presenting problem (one line); Case complexity. Reflective Exercise: EPA; Supervisor or mentor; Date of the case or incident; Reflecting on; What happened; What went well, and what could have gone differently; What I learned; What I will do differently; then Discussion. DOPS: EPA; Assessor; Date observed; Procedure; Clinical setting; Procedure complexity; then Entrustment ("Supervision required for this procedure") and Feedback. Portfolio review: EPA; Reviewer; Review period from; Review period to; Portfolio export (file name); Notes for your reviewer; then Review (Date of the review, Evidence reviewed, Review comments, Agreed actions).
  • Save draft (E3) stays on an existing draft's page: a save without a move.

3 · What DESIGN.md changes

SectionChange
§ Form systemAn activity form is a stack of sections, each a .detail-card one deep. Someone else's section is .form-section--locked with its head row (C14). Required fields carry * and aria-required. The action bar is .form-actions--moves: the move first and filled, Save draft outlined, Cancel last and quiet. Move labels and "to <name>" follow the rule in § 2. A move needing a field its mover cannot write is greyed with its reason. Below 641px every control is 44px and the bar stacks, the move on top.
§ Alerts, validation, empty statesA refusal is the summary (focus, links to inputs) plus each field's message, and the field names the summary. A predicted refusal is the field's message before any submit. A changed value drops a refusal's mark at once and the hints return. A move's outcome is the result region. Load failures follow Home's pattern; "unavailable" covers both a missing record and one the reader cannot open.
§ Page-level patternsA record page with a workflow opens with the status card, then .details-grid (About, then the sections), then the history (a table oldest first, notes on their own line; stacked behind <details> below 641px). About also holds the form's version when a reason names it, and the credit warnings.
