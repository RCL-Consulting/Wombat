A3 · The speciality's page holds its sub-specialities. Specialities lists the specialities, each naming its sub-specialities. A speciality's own page is its record (h2 sections, flow 12's user page) and the table of its sub-specialities with their ladders; a sub-speciality is a page of its own. The Sub-specialities list route redirects to its speciality's page (PROPOSED).

Why, and the cost

  • Scales to many specialities: the list stays one row each, and a speciality's page carries its own tree.
  • Edit, Sub-specialities and Back collapse into one link a row; each record keeps Status as an end section, as B2 does for a College.
  • Steps: reading the tree with ladders (1.12–1.14) 2 presses; 6.12 4 presses + 3 fields.
  • Cost: a new page shape for the speciality (record + table); two levels of page instead of one.
