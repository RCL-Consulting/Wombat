10 · Edge cases

  • CNSA has no College admin: B2's page says the Administrator keeps its catalogue meanwhile (R1-B2-3).
  • A College admin whose account names no College: today redirected to Colleges, which refuses him (T291). Proposed: Specialities shows "Your account names no College. Ask the platform administrator to add it." and no list.
  • A speciality with no sub-speciality: the tree's section reads "No sub-specialities yet. Add the first sub-speciality to Neurology." (A2, A3).
  • A sub-speciality's default ladder deleted elsewhere: refused while it is the default (6.8); C2 says so before.
  • A ladder whose rung a pinned item needs: C1-7 and C2-5.
  • A College, speciality or sub-speciality deactivated while an institution has adopted its curriculum: the adoption and its trainees carry on; the dialog says so and counts the institutions (DATA).
  • Two Colleges with similar names: every row, dialog and result uses the full name and the short code beside it in the list and subtitle.
  • A description of 2,000 characters: wraps in .col-wrap and in the textarea; the stacked row at 390 keeps it whole. Its limit is not given: ‹limit›.
