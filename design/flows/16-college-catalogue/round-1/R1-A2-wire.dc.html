Why, and the cost

  • The goal's "read the College's tree at a glance": one page shows which sub-specialities each speciality has and the ladder each uses. CPSA is one speciality and two sub-specialities; the heavy case (six under one speciality) is a six-row table.
  • Removes a page and its Back link; the default is chosen at create, so 6.12 loses a save.
  • Steps: reading the tree (1.12–1.14) 1 press; 6.12 3 presses + 3 fields. Cost: a new list shape (sections of tables), the result token on the tree, a redirect, and the create form gains a field.
  • Many specialities (not in the data): the sections grow long; a College with ten would want A3.
