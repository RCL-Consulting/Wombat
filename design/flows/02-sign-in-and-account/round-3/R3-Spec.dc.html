Focus rule for the sign-in slot (C6)

  • A refusal takes the focus (tabindex -1, autofocus) and both fields name it.
  • A notice with no institution buttons: Email takes the focus and names the notice (aria-describedby).
  • A notice with institution buttons: the notice itself takes the focus (tabindex -1, autofocus), so it is announced and the next Tab reaches the first institution.
  • No message: Email takes the focus when there are no buttons; nothing autofocuses when there are.
  • The notices show only to a signed-out visitor; a signed-in visitor who opens /account/login is sent Home (E4).

The six password rules (C2)

One heading, "The new password needs:", one order and one sentence per rule, in the list shown before typing, in the refusal, on register (F11) and on the administrator's reset card (F12, E11):

  • At least 12 characters.
  • At least 4 different characters. (RequiredUniqueChars = 4)
  • A digit (0 to 9).
  • An upper-case letter.
  • A lower-case letter.
  • A symbol, such as ! or #.

The refusal lists only the rules broken; the field says "The new password does not meet the rules below."

  • § Accessibility (required fields show *): dropped on this flow's forms. Every field on them is required, so a mark on each tells nothing; required exposes it to assistive technology, and the browser's own check names a blank field.
  • § Account / auth page:
    • "a 400px centred card" → the card is 30rem (480 px) as built, top-aligned, 16 px between its blocks (E8), padding 40 px, 24 px below 641 px.
    • The .mb-3 markup → FormField (.fld), and .password-field for a password.
    • "Change password stays an interactive page, for its Show buttons" → static, [ExcludeFromInteractiveRouting].
    • The PasswordToggleButton paragraph → the static toggle. PasswordToggleButton is used today by sign-in, register, link, change password and the administrator's reset card; all five move to the new toggle in this flow (E11), then it is deleted.
    • "?status=updated" → the redirect goes to /account/profile?status=password-updated.
    • "session ended … in its danger Alert" → an info Alert; the C6 focus rule.
    • 44 px controls below 641 px on this flow's pages, the auth card, My account and Change password (E7).
    • The sign-out page is drawn by both GETs, with its own form; the post requires the antiforgery token (E6).
