Forgotten password

A Wombat administrator can set a new password for you.

  1. Ask your Wombat administrator for a new password: the one at your institution or, if your account belongs to no institution, the platform's administrator. They give it to you themselves; Wombat does not email it.
  2. Sign in with it.
  3. Choose your own password on My account, under Change password.

If your institution signs you in, use its button on the sign-in page. Your institution resets that password, not Wombat.
