a · Blank

Reset password

Sets a new password directly. The user is not emailed; share the password securely out-of-band.

The new password needs:
  • At least 12 characters.
  • At least 4 different characters.
  • A digit (0 to 9).
  • An upper-case letter.
  • A lower-case letter.
  • A symbol, such as ! or #.

The card is interactive (the admin page is), so the toggle here is the same markup driven by the page; its minlength="8" and "disabled until 8 characters" go, since the rule is 12 and the refusal names every rule.
