The password toggle (NEW: .password-field, .password-toggle)

A button beside its input, never inside it. One markup on the four pages (sign in, change password ×3, link); shown and wired by wombat.js on [data-password-toggle], rendered hidden until then. The visible word stays "Show"; the state is the fill, the icon and aria-pressed.

a · At rest. Outline button: secondary-color label and edge on the surface, 4.86:1. 38 × 84 px.

b · The input focused. The ring (focus-ring, 2 px at 2 px) sits in the 8 px gap and does not touch the toggle.

c · The toggle focused, not pressed. Screen reader: "Show password, toggle button, not pressed".

d · Pressed and focused. Filled: on-fill on secondary-color, 4.86:1; eye-off; the input's type is text. The ring stays focus-ring on the surface, 4.86:1, 2 px clear of the fill.

e · Pressed, the input focused again. Pressing the toggle leaves the focus on it; the person moves back to type. Posting the form sets every field back to hidden (the page reloads).

f · Scripts blocked. No toggle at all (rendered hidden); the field fills the row. Nothing dead to press.

g · Below 641 px. Field and toggle 44 px tall.

Names on each page Sign in, link: "Show password". Change password: "Show current password", "Show new password", "Show new password again". Each name starts with the visible word (WCAG 2.5.3); aria-controls names its input; aria-pressed="false" or "true". The icon is decorative. wombat.js On load: for each [data-password-toggle], remove hidden, listen for click (no inline onclick; the CSP forbids it), flip the input's type and aria-pressed, swap the icon's use. About 20 lines; no framework.
