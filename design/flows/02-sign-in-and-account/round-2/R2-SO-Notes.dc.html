Sign out · notes

Two addresses, one page

  • GET /account/logout and GET /account/logout-confirm both draw this page: the same markup, its own <form method="post" action="/account/logout"> and a fresh antiforgery token. Neither redirects, and no GET signs out (T317: no more 405).
  • /account/logout-confirm is not reached only by typing: flow 01's error page links its Sign out here, because a form on the error page would carry a token its own failed request invalidated. So it must stay a page that draws its own form, never a redirect to a GET that signs out.
  • POST /account/logout signs out and lands on Sign in with "You have signed out." The audit log records a Logout (A.4.7).

The page

  • The anonymous card, no lockup (the lockup stays the sign-in card's), no nav: signed in, but nothing else on the page should be pressed. Static ([ExcludeFromInteractiveRouting]).
  • Names who is signed in, as stored, with the email, so the next person at a shared ward computer sees whose session it is.
  • Cancel is a link to the returnUrl when it is a local path (the error page passes its own), else Home (A.4.7: his dashboard).
  • h1 and tab "Sign out · Wombat". The focus starts at the top.
