What changes in DESIGN.md

  • § Account / auth page: the account page's heading is its <h1> (today an <h2>), at account-title 2rem. One message slot under it. Notices (session ended, password changed, signed out) are an info Alert with role="status" and leave the focus on the first field, which names them; refusals stay a danger Alert that takes the focus. "Session ended … in its danger Alert" goes.
  • § Account / auth page: the purpose line under the lockup (.account-purpose, muted text). The SSO block above the form: .sso-providers of .sso-button links, then .sso-divider "or sign in with your email and password"; nothing when no provider is configured; no autofocus when it is.
  • § Account / auth page: PasswordToggleButton (@onclick) is retired for the static .password-field / .password-toggle, wired by wombat.js. .password-wrapper and .password-toggle-btn go.
  • § Account / auth page: below 641 px the card's padding is space-lg (as --wide already is) and the ground's side padding 16 px; controls and buttons 44 px, the primary full width.
  • § Account / auth page: the sign-out page is the anonymous card with its own form, drawn by GET /account/logout and GET /account/logout-confirm alike (T317); never a redirect.
  • § Account / auth page: Change password carries [ExcludeFromInteractiveRouting]; its success redirects to /account/profile?status=password-updated.
  • § Page-level patterns (Account): My account is three cards, Account (details list), Your name (the one form), How you sign in (method rows); its email is text, not a disabled field; roles by label, one per line. Removing an institutional sign-in is a form post from a ConfirmDialog to a new endpoint that issues the sign-in again.
  • ProfileOutcome NameMissing → FirstNameMissing, LastNameMissing. SignInOutcome + SignedOut. SignInMessages, ExternalLoginRefusal, LinkExternalOutcome, PasswordRuleMessages: the new words above.
  • The account page's required fields carry no asterisk: every field on these forms is required.
