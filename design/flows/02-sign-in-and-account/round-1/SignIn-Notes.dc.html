Pages 1–2 · Sign in

Static: no circuit, no live validation. The page's words come from the code in the address.

1

The lockup: the mark at 56 px and the wordmark. Unchanged.

2

The purpose line (Q1): one sentence, no College or institution name.

3

One message slot, under the h1 and above everything the person acts on. A refusal (danger, role="alert", takes the focus) or a notice (info, role="status", the focus stays on Email). Never both, never stacked. It grows with its sentence.

4

The password toggle is a button beside the field, not drawn inside it: it can be 44 px on a phone, and it leaves the field's right edge to password managers, which put their own icons there. Visible "Show" with the eye icon; accessible name "Show password"; aria-pressed; aria-controls="password". It is rendered hidden and shown and wired by wombat.js (data-password-toggle, no inline onclick), so with scripts blocked there is no dead button. Pressed, it draws its pressed state and the icon turns to eye-off; the words stay "Show", so the name keeps its visible label.

5

Sign in posts the form to /account/login/submit with its antiforgery token and the returnUrl in a hidden field. Right-aligned on desktop, full width on a phone.

6

"Forgotten your password?" leads to page 6. One link, no "Reset it": nothing is reset there (Q2 A).

7

SSO (page 2): one link per configured provider, to /account/sso-challenge/{providerKey}, named "Sign in with" and the provider's display name, the building-2 icon. Full width, 44 px, wraps a long name. No provider, no block: A.3.2's page is the plain form.

8

The divider's words say what the form is for. In option A: "or sign in with your email and password"; in B, "or".

Focus on arrival

Blank: Email (autofocus, as today). A refusal: the refusal. A notice: Email, which names the notice. The tab is "Sign in · Wombat", and "Error: Sign in · Wombat" after a refusal only.

What the person keeps

After a refusal both fields are empty: the redirect carries only the code, never the address typed (it would sit in logs and history). Signing in after "session ended" returns to the page the tab was on; after a typed address, to that address (A.4.7). The returnUrl (a local path only) rides in a hidden field and on every redirect back to this page, refusals included, so a refusal between notice and success does not lose it (A.4.3: session ended, old password refused, new password, back to her inbox). Reached from a protected address, the page shows no message: it is the blank state.
