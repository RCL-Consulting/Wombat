What the proposal changes in DESIGN.md

  • § Account / auth page: "session ended … in its danger Alert" becomes an info Alert (role="status"); password changed and signed out likewise. Refusals stay danger. One slot under the h1.
  • § Account / auth page: PasswordToggleButton (@onclick; dead signed out) is retired for one static toggle: markup plus a wombat.js handler on data-password-toggle, rendered hidden and shown by the script, so a page with scripts blocked has no dead button.
  • § Account / auth page: Change password gains [ExcludeFromInteractiveRouting] and is static in the shell, as /Error is. Its success redirect goes to /account/profile?status=password-updated.
  • § Account / auth page: the sign-out confirmation moves from PageHeader + .form-container to the anonymous card, and answers GET /account/logout (T317).
  • § Page-level patterns: a refusal on Change password moves from under the page header into the form's card, above its action row, as flow 01's outcome rule has it for every form. A result from another page (Password updated) stays under the header.
  • ProfileOutcome: NameMissing splits in two. The email leaves the name form; it is shown once, as text, in the Account card.
  • SignInOutcome: a new notice code, SignedOut.
  • Nothing changes in the shell, the lockup rule (the lockup only on the sign-in card) or the tokens.

NEW:

  • .auth-divider: the "or" rule between the institution buttons and the form.
  • SSO button: a full-width outline button with building-2 (.btn-block, NEW modifier).
  • Rules list: the password rules under New password, linked by aria-describedby (a field-help list).
  • Sign-in method row: a method, its detail, its action (Change password, Remove) or its reason in words.

Edge cases

  • A 40-character email (thandi.zulu@paediatrics.kgk.wombat.local): wraps anywhere in the Account card and in the link page's sentence. The top bar shows the name; an account with no name shows the email cut at 28 characters, its full text in the link's accessible name.
  • A long refusal: the slot grows; nothing sits beside it. The longest today is 38 words, over the brief's 25: at 390 px about ten lines.
  • Four roles: one per line in the Account card, no truncation, no "and 2 more".
  • Three providers: a stack of full-width buttons, 44 px on a phone; a 40-character institution name wraps inside its button.
  • Institutional-only account: no Change password link anywhere; a typed address shows the info panel.
  • Refused sign-in comes back with empty fields: the redirect carries only the code, never the address typed.

Accessibility review (WCAG 2.1 AA, structural)

  • Contrast: nothing new is painted outside flow 01's measured pairs: text and muted text on the surface, text on the four tints (11.2 to 11.9:1), input-border 3.80:1, focus ring 4.86:1. Round 2 measures the SSO button, the divider's words and the pressed toggle.
  • Targets: every button and link 36 px or more on desktop (the toggle 38 px, beside its input); 44 px on a phone, including the toggle, Remember me's row and "Forgotten your password?".
  • The toggle: visible "Show" and an eye; accessible name "Show password", "Show current password", "Show new password", "Show new password again"; aria-pressed="false|true"; aria-controls the input. The name holds "Show" (2.5.3 label in name); the pressed state is drawn, not only announced.
  • Focus: a refusal takes it (all pages); a notice leaves it on the first field, which names the notice; a success takes it; the browser's check keeps its own; the Sign out, Forgotten password and Link-expired pages start at the top.
  • Static pages work with scripts blocked: every action is a link or a form post, and the toggle is simply absent.
  • Error pages' tab titles start "Error:"; notices do not.
