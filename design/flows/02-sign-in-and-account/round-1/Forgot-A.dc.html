Forgotten password

1

An administrator at your institution can set a new password for you.

  1. Ask your institution's Wombat administrator for a new password. They give it to you themselves; Wombat does not email it.
  2. Sign in with it.
  3. Choose your own password under My account, Change password.
2

If your institution signs you in, use its button on the sign-in page. Your institution resets that password, not Wombat.
