Page 4 · My account

Inside the shell. Reached from the name in the top bar, which is the current page (underlined, aria-current="page"); no nav item lights; no trail. Tab "My account · Wombat". Interactive: it loads the account in its circuit. Its one form posts to /account/profile/submit, which saves the name, issues the sign-in again and redirects with ?status= or ?error=.

1

The top bar's name is the way in and the "you are here" mark. Unchanged from flow 01.

2

Account: read-only facts, as a details list. The email once, as text (no longer a disabled field in the form, which repeated it at low contrast). The institution, for an account that has one; the row is left out for a platform or College account. The roles by label, one per line, in the precedence order. The subtitle names what the page holds.

3

Your name: the only form. First and last name side by side from 641 px (.form-grid), required, maxlength 100. "Save name". Its result (saved, or a refusal naming the field) shows in this card above the action row and takes the focus as the page loads; a refused field is marked (border, stripe, message with the circle-alert icon) and names the refusal.

4

How you sign in (Q4, T286): one row per way in. Password, with Change password (a link to page 5). Each linked institutional sign-in, with its date and Remove. The institutional part appears only where the person's institution has a provider: on dev, where none is configured, the card holds the password row alone.

5

Data volumes: a 40-character email wraps anywhere; four roles are four lines. The top bar shows the name; only an account with no name shows the email there, cut at 28 characters, the whole address in the link's accessible name.

6

An institutional-only account: no Change password, and a sentence saying why. Remove on the last way in is disabled, its reason in visible words beginning with its name ("Remove: this is the only way you sign in."). With a password as well, Remove is enabled and asks first (ConfirmDialog: "Remove your [institution] sign-in?" / "You will sign in with your Wombat password only. You can link it again the next time you use your institution's sign-in."). Listing and removing links are not built.

Layout

Two columns from 901 px, Account (1fr) beside Your name over How you sign in (2fr), as .details-grid; one column below, in the same order. The acting role changes nothing on this page.

Not on this page

No email change (the email is the account's identity; an administrator changes it, F12). No acting-role switch (the sidebar has it). No data-rights links (My data rights is in the nav).
