# Flow 11 round 2: the design-system review

Reviewed 2026-10-19 from the design-system side: the boards in `round-2/project/` (R2-G01 to G18 the register page and its
cards, L01 to L17 Invitations, M01 to M11 Admit, N01 to N23 Invite a person, P01 to P06 the pending registrar's Home, T01
to T10 Trainees, X01; 1280 and 390 twins, 768 for six; plus R2-0-spec, R2-0-spec-2 and R2-0-steps) and `r2.css`, against
`design/system/` (version 25: README, `components/*/README.md`, `components/bundle.css`, `tokens.json`),
`execution/architecture/DESIGN.md` and `src/Wombat.Web/wwwroot/app.css`. Model: flow 10's `round-2-review-ds.md`.

Method. Static first: scripts over every board (classes against `bundle.css` and `r2.css`, raw colours, `var(--…)` against
the tokens, ids and `aria-*` references, headings, trails, the lit menu item, inline styles, 1280 against 390 and 768 text).
**Then rendered**: the boards link `ds/wombat/tokens.css` and `components/bundle.css`, which the folder does not hold, so I
served a scratch copy (tokens from `app.css` `:root`, `bundle.css` v25, the boards, `r2.css`) and measured in Chromium:
horizontal overflow at 1280, 1100, 1000, 901, 900, 768, 700, 641, 640 and 390; every control's box at 390; the table
containers' scroll widths. Sizes below are measured unless marked "computed". The cast, DTOs and code are other reviewers'
remit. Board text is read as data. Severity: M must-fix, S should-fix, N nit.

## What holds

- **Colour and tokens.** No raw hex, `rgb()` or `hsl()` on any board or in `r2.css`; every `var(--…)` is defined in
  `:root`; no new token. The spec's contrast figures recompute (muted on surface 5.09, secondary 4.86, warning icon on
  warning-bg 5.43, focus ring 4.86). `.delivery--sending` muted on the surface, `.inv-count` muted on the page ground (4.83)
  and `.otl-copied` (`on-fill` on `secondary-color`, 4.86) pass.
- **Classes.** Every class a board uses is in `bundle.css` or `r2.css`; none is missing. Component classes as their records
  say: `.dialog-card`/`.dialog-body`/`.dialog-list` (action mode, safe button first, danger fill on the action alone),
  `.password-field`/`.password-toggle`/`.password-rules`, `.account-*`, `.breadcrumb-*`, `.actions-cell`, `.skeleton`,
  `.alert-*` with the right default roles (success and warning `status`, danger `alert`, info `status` for a notice that
  arrives with the page).
- **Tables carry both stack classes** (`clinic-table--stack clinic-table--stack-wide`), `th scope="col"`, a visually hidden
  "Actions" header, `th scope="row"` on the invitee/name, `data-label` on every cell that does not say what it is, and
  row buttons whose `aria-label` starts with the visible word and names the row. They stack at 900 and below with no
  sideways scroll (measured 0 at 900, 768, 700, 641, 640, 390).
- **Shell.** Institutional admin's, Administrator's and Pending trainee's menus equal DESIGN.md § The NavMenu row for row.
  Lists light themselves `aria-current="page"` (Invitations, Trainees, Home); Invite a person and Admit light their owner
  `"true"`; the trail is Home › owner › page and its last crumb, the h1 and the tab stem are the same words on all 18
  record/form boards; the Administrator's Trainees (T06) lights nothing and its trail is Home › Trainees, as the owner
  table says. A refused post's tab starts "Error: " (G04–G06, M09, N02, N10, N11, N13–N18).
- **Headings.** One h1 per page (the dialog stage L06 has none, as a stage), no skipped level, no `tabindex` on an h1, no
  duplicate id, no dangling `aria-labelledby`/`-describedby`/`for`.
- **Voice.** No "Please", sentence case throughout, ISO dates, "SAST" times, roles by label (the only code-style role
  names are in the Steps board, which quotes today's words), results lead with a bold first sentence (L07, L10, N06, T02–T04).
- **1280/768/390 twins** carry the same text and markup in `<main>` on every board; the 390 boards' only different markup is
  inline style (D17).
- **390 boards:** G (all 18), P (all 6), T and the tables never scroll sideways, and every control in them is 44 px
  except the findings below. 768 boards: nothing scrolls, targets 36/28 px.
