  • GetInvitationPreview: the institution or College name, the speciality and sub-speciality names, ExpiresOn (Register's info line and its note).
  • IssueInvitation: an account with the address; an active invitation to it; each judged against the caller's scope (T286 / Q12, Q10).
  • ListActiveInvitations: the expired, unused ones of the last 30 days (Q9); the resend's time (ResentOn, L10).
  • GetTraineeDashboardSummary: the used invitation's issuer, role and date (found by her address and UsedOn); her scope names; the curriculum when exactly one version is adopted.
  • ListPendingTrainees: the speciality and sub-speciality names; Registered from the used invitation's UsedOn ("—" with none); the institution for the Administrator.
  • ListTraineesForSpeciality: the programme start; TrainingYear.
  • The Admit page: the training year for a chosen start (on the date's change, one round trip); the derived completion; the registrar's invited scope and registration date.
  • AdmitTrainee: Q7's mail (decided, T367).
  • wombat.js: the clipboard call behind Copy link; the old clearInvitationTokenFromUrl (wombat.js:3) goes.
  • Q8: the count waiting, for flow 12.

Build notes

  • C5, the token cookie: GET /account/register?token=… checks the token, writes it to a cookie (SameSite=Lax, protected by Data Protection, HttpOnly, Secure, Path=/account/register, 30 minutes) and answers 303 to /account/register. The submit reads it; registering, or any refused-link card, deletes it. A refused registration's redirect carries neither the token nor the codes (T341: the cookie carries them). No novalidate on the form (C7).
  • A signed-in visitor's pages are interactive (App.razor:110-114): Q11's card and the cookie's redirect must be served static for them too.
  • Cookie lapsed (G16): no Try again button: the invitation is gone from the browser, and a button cannot carry the token; the card says to open the email's link again. If you want the button, it can only reopen this card.
  • C3, the sweep: accepting one invitation revokes every other active invitation to that address, other institutions' included (AcceptInvitation.cs:99-113, T061). Two admins inviting one registrar: whichever link is used, the other stops working; Q10 now refuses the second at issue.
  • The Admit page's training year is one round trip on the date's change (onchange), never per keystroke.

Edge cases

  • A 64-character address breaks after its @ (N21, and every Invitee cell).
  • A refused issue after an issue: no link remains (N10, N11).
  • Two invitations to one address: refused at issue (N17, N18); a registrar invited by two admins: the second refused, and the sweep (C3) for any that slipped through.
  • An expired, unused invitation: Expired, not used, Invite again (L14, N20).
  • The Administrator admitting: the institution kept (T06); an institution with nothing adopted: M04; a second adopted version: M05.
  • A future start: Not started (M06); a start over four years back: Training year 5 and a warning (M07).
  • A signed-in visitor on someone's link: G18; Register with script blocked: G17.
