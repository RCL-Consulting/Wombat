Screen 1 · Invitations: the three variations

A · Issue above the list

One page, as today, re-stacked: the result and its one-time link, then the issue form at full width, then the list at full width.

Why: keeps today's single page and the list in view as each issue lands; the form gets the width a scope row needs, and the table gets the page's full width, so Delivery and the actions fit at 1280.

Components: PageHeader; FormField / FormActions (.form-container, .form-grid); OneTimeLink NEW; Alert, ActionResult; DataTable with both stacks (.clinic-table--stack, --stack-wide) and a note row NEW (tr.inv-note); ConfirmDialog; StatePanel; Skeleton; Icon (mail NEW, user-plus NEW, hourglass NEW, graduation-cap NEW, refresh, copy, send, circle-alert, circle-check, clock).

DESIGN.md: § The invitations list (columns and Delivery), § Form system (fields shown per role, not disabled), § Button system's destructive action (Revoke outline + confirmation), § Table system (a note row under its row).

Routes: none new.

B · A page of its own

Invitations is the list alone, with "Invite a person" in its header. Invite a person PROPOSED (/admin/invitations/new) is a form page: role as radios with what each role does, the scope fields it takes, Issue invitation. After an issue it stays, the link above a reset form headed "Invite the next person".

Why: Home's "Invite a person" lands on the form itself (as flow 10 gave New MSF campaign); nine invitations in a row never scroll past a list; the list page is only the list, the widest table of the three.

Components: as A, plus a form page of its own (flow 08's pattern) and choice-group radios (flow 08's) for Role.

DESIGN.md: as A, plus § Page-level patterns: Form page (a new form page under Invitations, the title rule: "Invite a person" is the h1, the crumb and the tab stem; Invitations stays lit, aria-current="true").

Routes: /admin/invitations/new PROPOSED; the revoke result's "Invite another address…" opens it with the role and scope filled PROPOSED.

C · Beside the list, as rows

Today's arrangement, kept but rebuilt: the list in the wide column as rows, not a ten-column table; the form in a 22rem column beside it, the link at its head. A resend's new link opens in its own row; a revoked row stays as its result until the next action.

Answers to the questions

  1. Where issuing lives: three answers drawn, B recommended. All three show Delivery and the actions at 1280.
  2. The link: in OneTimeLink, with Copy link; it is cleared by the next action of any kind and never reaches the URL.
  3. The form: fields per role, not disabled ones; no role by default; the only institution shown as text; refusals under the field.
  4. The wait: H1.
  5. Admission: W1 recommended; the training year beside the start; two lists.
  6. Register: Sign in for used and existing; ask for a new invitation for revoked, expired, invalid; the card carries the lockup and purpose.
  7. Q7–Q11 are yours: drawn where they would show, each marked.

Edge cases

  • A 64-character address breaks after its @, then anywhere; never widens a column (S3).
  • A refused issue after an issue: the link is gone before the refusal shows (A2, B2, C2).
  • Two invitations to one address, or one registrar invited by two admins: Q10's refusal beside Email; today both stay active, and the first used spends only itself.
  • An invitation that expires unused leaves the list (today); Q9 is the alternative.
  • An Administrator admitting a registrar with no institution: refused, "An institution is required." on the issue side; on admission the page names the registrar's institution or says it has none and offers no Admit.
  • Nothing adopted: the admission's built words, with Curriculum adoptions linked; no Admit.
  • A second adopted version: only the adopted one offered (T091, T304).
  • A start in the future: "Training year 1 · the programme starts on <date>"; nothing can be filed before it.
  • A start more than four years back: the year still shown (training year 5 or more), with a warning, not a refusal (.field-warning).
  • A signed-in visitor on someone's link: Q11's card.
  • Register with script blocked: the Show toggles are hidden; the token still leaves the address bar (the redirect needs no script).

Accessibility review (WCAG 2.1 AA)

  • One h1 a page; then h2 (Invite a person, Active invitations); the dialog's title an h2 in the top layer. Every table captioned, never repeating its heading.
  • Every row action named for its row ("Revoke the Trainee invitation to ndlvou@kgk.wombat.local"); Resend described by its note row.
  • Focus: an issue or resend to the result; a refusal to its field; a revoke's dialog starts on Keep the invitation, Escape returns to the row's Revoke…, the action to the result; an admission to its result.
  • Refusals tied by aria-describedby and marked by the field's stripe, not colour alone.
  • Copy link announced through an aria-live line ("Link copied."); the button's state is in its words, not its colour.
  • Targets: 36px buttons and 28px small ones at 1280; 44px at 390 (row actions, radios, Copy link, links on the wait card).
  • Delivery's states are words with an icon; the warning stripe repeats, never replaces, the words.
  • Contrast: every pair from the system's table (text on the tints, muted on surface 5.09:1).
