# Curricula

Curricula (`/admin/curricula`, `Pages/Admin/Curricula/CurriculaList.razor`; its words `CurriculaWords.cs`; the reads `GetCurriculaListQuery` (with `CurriculumDto.Institution`, a version's state for one institution) and `GetAdoptableCurriculaQuery`, the adoption `AdoptCurriculumCommand`, `AdoptionRefusedException`; the addresses `Components/Shared/CatalogueLinks.cs`): **the College's curriculum versions, and for an institution which of them it trains against and which it may adopt.** Flow 15 (T392, 2026-11-02, c931d56f; boards R3-A01 to A15, R2-A12; Q1, Q6, Q7, Q10, Q18, Q21, S1, S2, S7, S15, S19, F5, T9; build review C4, P1, P8, P9) made it the one page for adoption. Until then an institution adopted on a page of its own, Curriculum adoptions, from a picker that offered the version it had already adopted (which Adopt then refused), superseding without asking and printing the exception's text on a failure; Curricula printed the College's `IsActive` as "Active", so KGK's superseded 11.1 read "Active". Flow 16 (T394, 2026-11-05, de4cc718, with f7cb1903, c6bbb641 and 2c93b247; boards R3-F01, R3-D01; F01, T326, Q20) named the College in the College admin's subtitle and caption, sent Edit to a version's own page (CurriculumRecord), gave the one-word columns their content's width and the text columns the room, and moved the stack to 1150px.

`/admin/adoptions` (`Pages/Admin/Adoptions/AdoptionsList.razor`) is no menu item since flow 15: it keeps its policy, sends to `/admin/curricula` replacing itself in the history, and draws nothing (its tab reads "Curricula · Wombat" for the moment before it does). NavOwners lights Curricula for it.

## Who reads it

`[Authorize(Policy = "NationalCatalogueAccess")]`; NavMenu: Curricula (`book-open`) in the Administrator's Catalogue group, the College admin's one ungrouped list, and the Institutional admin's Curriculum group. It is a list, so it lights itself and has no trail. **What it draws depends on the reader**, never on a parameter:

- **An Institutional admin** (Prof Nolwazi Mbatha at Kgosi Kgari Teaching Hospital): Curricula you train against, then Open for adoption.
- **A College admin** (Dr Anton Kruger, CPSA): the College's versions in one table, no section heading.
- **The Administrator**: All curricula, then An institution's curricula.

## States

- **Loading** (S19): `p.loading-line[role="status"]` "Loading the curricula.", then, for an institution and the Administrator, each section's heading over a skeleton card; no Adopt until the read is in. The College admin's is the line alone.
- **Load failed** (S7, R3-A15): "Could not load the curricula. Nothing has changed. Try again, or come back in a few minutes." with Try again (StatePanel, `refresh-cw`), which focuses the h1 once it reads; no empty state under it. The cause goes to the log (T329).
- **Show failed** (P9): inside the section, an `ActionResult#cur-inst-error` holding a danger `Alert` with `.alert-row`, the same sentence and an outline "Try again" with `refresh-cw`, which reads the chosen institution again; the failure takes the focus. Show while it reads: the loading line.

## Reads and focus

The first read and Try again draw the loading state; an adoption, Show and Try again in the section answer in place. An adoption's result takes the focus (`ActionResult`, at the top); a refusal or failure takes it in the open section; Show's answer focuses its first h3; a refused Show focuses the select. Every adoption, refused or not, reads the list again, the Administrator's chosen institution kept.

## Narrow widths

**From 1151px the tables are tables**: on the College's and the Administrator's lists (`.curricula-catalogue`) Curriculum takes 30% and College and Discipline 18% each, and Effective, Items and Status fit their content (`.col-fit--wide`, f7cb1903, c6bbb641: at the shared widths Effective broke its day and "Held back" wrapped); an institution's two tables keep the shared widths with Effective fitted (and Open for adoption's Items), since their Items sentence ("15 national · 1 your own") and the adoption line need the room (2c93b247 fitted Effective again after c6bbb641 dropped it). **At 1150px and below every table stacks** (flow 16's shared part in app.css, with the items list; DataTable): the build's sweep found the Administrator's Curricula 756px wide in a 717px container at 1025px, and the replay an institution's 17px over at 1101px. Stacked, each version is a block: the curriculum first (with A2's line under it in Open for adoption), each other cell labelled ("Discipline:", "Effective:", "Items:", "Status:", "For your institution:"), its buttons last. Below 641px `.people-page` makes every button, input and select 44px, and the filter's Show is the row's width (`.filter-actions .btn`); the dialog's buttons stack with Adopt 11.2 on top (`.dialog-card .form-actions`, the system rule since flow 11). app.css's "Flow 15: curricula" holds `.curricula-section`'s column and its spacing (`.action-result:empty` takes no row; the chosen institution's sections 8px further apart); the widths are in "Flow 15: EPAs" (the 1025px and 1151px queries flow 16 added there) and the stack in flow 16's shared part. Measured on flow 16's replay as the College admin, the Administrator and the Institutional admin at 390 to 1440, the 1150px edge included: nothing over its container.
