# AdminEpaPage

An EPA's page in the catalogue (`/admin/epas/new`, `/admin/epas/{Id}`, `Pages/Admin/Epas/EpaEdit.razor`; its words `EpaWords.cs`, the fields' refusals `EpaFieldMessages` in `CreateEpa.cs`; the read `GetEpaPageQuery`, `EpaPageDto`; the commands `CreateEpaCommand`, `UpdateEpaCommand`, `DeactivateEpaCommand`; refusals under fields through `Components/Shared/FieldRefusal.cs` and `FieldRefusalException`; the result token `CatalogueLinks`): **one EPA as the catalogue keeps it: a form where the caller may change it, a record where they may not.** Not the trainee's EPA page (EpaPage, flow 05), which reads a registrar's standing on an EPA. Flow 15 (T392, 2026-11-02, c931d56f; boards R3-C08, C12, C18a, C18b and R2-C04 to C07, C10, C11, C13, C14, C19; Q2, Q11, Q12, Q19, F1, F4, F9, M5, S4, S8, S11, S19, T7; build review P4, N4, N5) rebuilt it. Until then it was "Edit EPA" for every EPA, a national one too (whose save `UpdateEpa` refused, T291), offered Back to EPAs, put a refusal at the top in a validation summary, printed an exception's text (T272, T329), and said "every institution that uses it" when deactivating an institution's own EPA. Flow 16 (T394, 2026-11-05, de4cc718; boards R3-E05 to E14, E20; Q2, Q21's EV2, S15, N16, N20, M4; fix pass S2, S5) gave the College's and the Administrator's form of a national EPA its College and discipline in the subtitle and its reach in a standing line and in the pause dialog, the required line, the Status group's legend, and Save's question returning the focus to Save.

## Who reads it

Administrator, College admin and Institutional admin (`NationalCatalogueAccess`), each reading by the EPA list's rule (Epas): an id outside it, and an unknown one, are **EPA unavailable** alike (Q2, Q19), so the Demo College's EPA-001 typed by Kgosi Kgari's admin is never a form. Reached from a row's link or Edit on EPAs, or Create EPA; EPAs is lit, and the trail is Home › EPAs › the EPA's code.

## Refusals under their field (F4, F9; DESIGN.md § Form system)

Each is a `div.validation-message[role="alert"]` with the id `‹input›-refusal`, named first in the control's `aria-describedby` (then its help), the control `aria-invalid` (by the form's own message store, so `InputBase` sets it), the first refused field focused, what was typed kept. No `ValidationSummary`, no `RefusalSummary`. Any other failure is fixed words in the result region, focused, the cause logged (T329): "The EPA could not be saved. Nothing has changed. Try again, or come back in a few minutes." (R2-C06).

## Deactivate (Q11, S8, S11; T158, T196, T312)

Deactivate…, and Save with Active unticked on an EPA stored active, open **one** `ConfirmDialog` (action mode, so it opens on Cancel), named for the EPA: "Deactivate PAED-012?" over, on a national EPA for the College admin or the Administrator, the reach as its own paragraph (`BodyContent`, R3-E07: "PAED-012 is on 1 curriculum adopted by 1 institution, Kgosi Kgari Teaching Hospital; 3 registrars in training are measured against it."), then "It can no longer be chosen for a new activity, and it stops being a target on running programmes' progress pages and dashboards, for every institution that has adopted it. ("… for Kgosi Kgari Teaching Hospital's trainees." for an own EPA); **Deactivate**, the filled danger button. Cancel or Escape returns the focus to the control that asked: Deactivate… after Deactivate…, Save after Save (`ConfirmDialog.ShowAsync(returnFocusId)`, flow 16's fix pass S2; until then Save's question returned it to Deactivate…); a Save it interrupted leaves Active unticked, unsaved, and Save asks again. Confirmed from Save, it saves every edit with the deactivation.

## Results (T234, F1, M5)

One result at a time, in the `ActionResult` at the form's top, focused once the action has answered and any dialog has closed: "EPA saved.", "EPA saved and deactivated.", "EPA deactivated." (Active unticked, Deactivate… gone), or a reactivation's count, "EPA reactivated. 1 activity completed against it while it was inactive now counts towards progress." ("… No activity was completed against it while it was inactive, so no progress changed." for none; T196, D48). **A create leaves for its new EPA's page** by `?created` (`CatalogueLinks.EpaCreated`): that page writes "EPA saved." itself, focuses it, then replaces its address without the token, so a reload says nothing. A failed deactivation: "The EPA could not be deactivated. Nothing has changed. Try again, or come back in a few minutes.", focused once the dialog has closed (build review P4).
