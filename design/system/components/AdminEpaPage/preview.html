PAED-012 — Communicating with and counselling patients, caregivers and healthcare teams

College of Paediatricians of South Africa · Paediatrics / Paediatrics · Set by the College, so read only here

Paediatrics / Paediatrics

Description

This EPA focuses on sharing clear health information with patients and their families, and with colleagues across the healthcare team. The registrar must convey medical information in an easy-to-understand way, listen well, and work effectively as a team member.

Required knowledge and skills

12.1 Communicate effectively with patients and families in emergency settings 12.2 Communicate effectively with patients and families in the neonatal ICU, neonatal high care and PICU 12.3 Counsel caregivers about immunisation, growth charts and dysmorphism 12.4 Demonstrate counselling for common presentations 12.5 Lead a counselling session with the family of a child with an LTHC 12.6 Demonstrate counselling in palliative care scenarios 12.7 Communicate effectively with healthcare colleagues
