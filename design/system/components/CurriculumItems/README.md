# CurriculumItems

A curriculum's items (`/admin/curricula/{Id}/items`, `Pages/Admin/Curricula/CurriculumItemsEdit.razor`; its words `CurriculumItemsWords.cs`, shared with the item's page; the reads `GetCurriculumByIdQuery` (with `CurriculumDto.Institution` and `MayAddItem`) and `ListCurriculumItemEpaOptionsQuery` (is an EPA left to add), the remove `RemoveCurriculumItemCommand`; the addresses and one-shot tokens `Components/Shared/CatalogueLinks.cs`): **the items of one curriculum version, what each asks of a registrar, and whose each is.** Flow 15 (T392, 2026-11-02, c931d56f with e10a5f22; boards R3-B01 to B03, B06 to B08, B10, B11, B13, B14, B17 to B21, B23, R2-B16; Q14, Q19, T3, T5, T7, T10, T11, F1, F3, F6, P2, M1, S10, S12, decisions 1 to 3) made **the list the page**. Until then it held the item's add and edit form in the table (T176's edit row, a second row spanning the table, which overflowed at 390, T323), offered Back links, and gave an institution no way to read a national item's whole setting.

## Who reads it

`[Authorize(Policy = "NationalCatalogueAccess")]`, reached by a version's Items on Curricula. Under Curricula in the trail (Home › Curricula › Items of Paediatric EPA Curriculum (11.1)), Curricula lit; no Back links (T5). **What a reader may change is the query's, never the page's**: each row carries `CurriculumItemDto.CanEdit`, the version `CanEditCurriculum` and `MayAddItem` (`CurriculumAdminScope`, T211).

- **The College admin and the Administrator** write the College's items: every national row offers Edit and Remove…; the Administrator reads every institution's own items too, each named for its owner.
- **An Institutional admin** reads the College's items as records (View) and changes only her institution's own items, on a version her institution has adopted.

## The one-shot tokens (F1, M5, M6)

The item's page returns here with an id in the address, never a sentence (`CatalogueLinks`): `?added=31` and `?saved=31` write the result here, focused; `?back=31` (Cancel or Back from an item) writes nothing and focuses that row's Edit or View. The list reads the item again in the caller's scope: an id the curriculum does not hold for the caller (stale, or another institution's) writes and focuses nothing. Then the address is replaced without the token, so a reload says nothing.

## States

- **Loading** (S19, R3-B18): h1 "Curriculum items", `p.loading-line[role="status"]` "Loading this curriculum's items." over a skeleton card.
- **Load failed** (S7, R3-B19): "Could not load this curriculum's items. Nothing has changed. Try again, or come back in a few minutes." with Try again (StatePanel, `refresh-cw`), which focuses the h1 once it reads; no notice and no table under it. The cause goes to the log (T329).
- **No items**: "Items (0)" over the add link or its line, and no table.

## Narrow widths

**The items stack to 1024px, not 900px** (e10a5f22; the replay's A.7.10): from 901 to about 990px the six columns need about 690px in a 593px column, so Edit and Remove… sat past the column's edge. app.css's "Flow 15: a curriculum's items" repeats the stack-wide rules under `@media (max-width: 1024px)` for `.curriculum-items-page` (and the EPA list's `.epas-page`): the header row visually hidden, each item a block, the EPA first, each other cell labelled ("Target and decision:", "Minimum level:", "Minimum by training year:", "Tools:"), its row actions 44px. From 1025px it is a table (at 1280 it fits, 972 in 972). Below 641px the add link beside "Items (n)" is 44px (decision 2), `.people-page` makes every button 44px, and the dialog's buttons stack with Remove item on top. Measured on the replay at 390, 768, 901 to 1024 and 1280: nothing sideways.

## Contrast

No new colour token. The owner line, the decision line, the scale line, "(inactive: not in force)" and "Set by the College." in `muted-text`, 5.09:1 on the surface; the notices' words `text-color` on `warning-bg` (11.89:1) and on `info-bg`, as Alert's.
