# CollegeTree

The College's tree (`/admin/colleges/{CollegeId}/specialities`, `Pages/Admin/Institutions/SpecialitiesList.razor`; its words `CollegeTreeWords.cs`; the read `GetCollegeTreeQuery`, `CollegeTreeDto`, `CollegeTreeSpecialityDto`, `CollegeTreeSubSpecialityDto`; the addresses and the one-shot tokens `Components/Shared/CatalogueLinks.cs`): **one College's specialities, a section each, with its sub-specialities and the ladder each uses.** Flow 16 (T394, 2026-11-05, de4cc718, with a2878763 and 7d4c4490: the description's and the default scale's room at 1280; boards R3-A02, A04, A07, A12, A13b, A14b, A16b, A22 and R2-A10, A18a, A19a, A20a; Q1, Q7, Q9, S3, S7, S9, S12, S13, M5, M8, N2, N4, N6) rebuilt it. Until then it was a table of the College's specialities (Name, Description, Status), each with Edit and a Sub-specialities link to a list of its own, under a "Back to colleges" the College admin could not follow (T291 item 2); its empty state said "Create the first speciality for this college." and a failed read printed the exception's text.

Two older addresses lead here; both are covered below (The old addresses): `/admin/specialities` (`MySpecialitiesRedirect.razor`, the College admin's menu item) and a speciality's old Sub-specialities list, `/admin/specialities/{SpecialityId}/sub-specialities` (`SubSpecialitiesList.razor`), which sends to the speciality's section (`CatalogueLinks.CollegeTreeAt`).

## Who reads it

`[Authorize(Policy = "AdministratorOrCollegeAdmin")]`. **What it reads is one College**: `GetCollegeTreeQuery` answers null for an unknown College and for one the caller cannot open (`CanAccessCollege`), so a College admin reads only their own College's tree, and the Administrator any. Specialities and each speciality's sub-specialities come ordered by name.

- **A College admin** (Anton Kruger, the CPSA's): Specialities (`stethoscope`), the first link of their one ungrouped list (Specialities, EPAs, Curricula, Activity types, Entrustment scales), whose address `/admin/specialities` sends them here; or Specialities on Home's National catalogue card. Specialities is lit (`NavOwners`' `UnderTheCollege`), and the trail is Home › Specialities › College of Paediatricians of South Africa: the College is the page's own crumb.
- **The Administrator** (devadmin): from Colleges (`graduation-cap`, in their Organisations group), whose row offers Specialities, named "Specialities of College of Neurologists of South Africa". Colleges is lit, and the trail is Home › Colleges › College of Neurologists of South Africa › Specialities, the College's crumb opening its record.

No Back to colleges and no other link to Colleges (Q9, T291 item 2): the trail is the way back.

## The page

- **The header**: h1 and tab "Specialities" (`TreeHeading`). The subtitle, once the tree is read (`TreeSubtitle`, the College's name and short code, S9): "College of Paediatricians of South Africa (CPSA): its specialities, their sub-specialities and the ladder each uses." **Create speciality** (primary, `plus`, to `…/specialities/new`) only once the tree is read: not while it reads, not on a failure, not on College unavailable.
- **The column**: `<div class="people-page college-tree-page">` (flow 12's list column, its parts 24px apart, and its phone rules), `aria-busy` while the first read runs.
- **One result region** (S3): an `ActionResult` at the column's top, empty (and taking no row, `.people-page .action-result:empty`) but for a token's result (below).
- **A section per speciality** (Q1): `section.user-section`, named by its h2 (`aria-labelledby`), whose id is the tree's section id, `speciality-‹id›` (`CatalogueLinks.TreeSectionId`): the old Sub-specialities address and a sub-speciality's Cancel lead to it as `…/specialities#speciality-2`.
  - **Its head**, `div.form-section-head`: the h2, the speciality's name, then an `.actions-cell` with **Edit** (outline, small, to the speciality's record) and **Create sub-speciality** (outline, small), each named for the speciality (M5, M8): "Edit the speciality Paediatrics", "Create sub-speciality of Paediatrics".
  - **Its line**, a `p.muted` (`SpecialityLine`): the description and the status, "Specialist training in Paediatrics, and certificate training in its sub-specialities. · Active"; the status alone, "Active" or "Inactive", where it has no description. A deactivated speciality stays in the tree, its line ending "· Inactive", its Edit and Create sub-speciality still offered.
  - **No sub-speciality yet** (R3-A12): a `p.muted` in place of the table, "No sub-specialities yet. Create the first sub-speciality of Neurology."
  - **Its sub-specialities**: a `DataTable` with `StackWide`, captioned "Sub-specialities of Paediatrics" (N6: not the heading's words alone). Columns: **Sub-speciality**, the row header (`th scope="row"`, `.col-wrap`), the name; where another sub-speciality of the College has the same name, the speciality is added in brackets, "‹name› (‹speciality›)" (N4, `RowNames.Distinct`; within one speciality a name is unique, and no board draws the case); **Description** (`.col-wrap--wide`), drawn only where a row of the section has one (T239); **Default scale** (`.col-wrap--scale`), the ladder's name, "CPSA Paediatric Entrustment Scale v11.1", or "No default" (R3-A13b); **Status** (`.col-fit`), "Active" or "Inactive"; and the actions column (`.col-fit`, its header the `.visually-hidden` "Actions"), **Edit** (outline, small), named for its row: "Edit the sub-speciality Neonatology".
- **Results by token** (S13): a record page returns here with `?created=` or `?saved=` naming the record, `speciality:‹id›` or `sub:‹id›`. The tree, read in the caller's scope, writes the result in the region as a success `Alert`, its one sentence bold, and focuses it: "**Speciality Neurology created.**", "**Sub-speciality Neonatology created.**", "**Speciality saved.**", "**Sub-speciality saved.**". The token is then taken out of the address, replacing the history entry; the page reads again only when the College's id changes, so the replace does not read it again, and a reload says nothing. A record the tree does not hold (another College's, a stale id) writes nothing and focuses nothing, and its token goes all the same.
- **Empty** (R2-A10; StatePanel): "No specialities yet" over "Create the first speciality for College of Neurologists of South Africa.", no button in the card: Create speciality in the header is the way on.
- **College unavailable** (Q7, N2, R2-A18a): an unknown College's id, or another College's typed by a College admin, is never an empty list offering Create. h1 and tab "College unavailable", no subtitle, no Create speciality; DESIGN.md's one Unavailable markup, a `.detail-card.detail-card--empty` holding `div.state-panel-copy` "This College does not exist, or you cannot open it." and `div.form-actions` with one primary link, no icon: **Go to Specialities** for the College admin, **Go to Colleges** for the Administrator. The trail ends at the h1: Home › Specialities › College unavailable, Home › Colleges › College unavailable.

## The old addresses

- **`/admin/specialities`** (`MySpecialitiesRedirect.razor`, the page of NavItems' Specialities): it sends, replacing itself in the history, the Administrator to Colleges (`/admin/colleges`) and a College admin to their College's tree. A College admin whose account names no College (S12, R2-A21) reads h1 and tab "Specialities" and the empty state "No College on your account" over "Your account names no College. Ask the platform administrator to add it.", with no link (Colleges refuses a College admin, T291 item 2). While it decides: "Loading the specialities.". It is the menu item's own page, so it has no trail.
- **`/admin/specialities/{SpecialityId}/sub-specialities`** (`SubSpecialitiesList.razor`; Q1, T291 item 2): no page links it. It reads the speciality in the caller's scope (`GetSpecialityByIdQuery`) and sends, replacing itself, to the tree at the speciality's section (`CatalogueLinks.CollegeTreeAt`, `/admin/colleges/2/specialities#speciality-2`). An unknown speciality, or another College's, is **Speciality unavailable**: "This speciality does not exist, or you cannot open it." with Go to Specialities or Go to Colleges, in the same markup. While it reads, h1 "Specialities" over "Loading the specialities."; a read that throws, "Could not load the specialities. Nothing has changed. Try again, or come back in a few minutes." with Try again. Its column is `.user-page.sub-speciality-page`.

## Reads and focus

The first read draws the loading state. Try again (S7) focuses the h1 once it has read (`PageFocus.FocusHeadingAsync`). A token's result takes the focus in the result region. Nothing on the page acts in place: every action is a link to a record page, which returns here by its token.

## States

- **Loading** (R2-A19a): `p.loading-line[role="status"]` "Loading the specialities." over one skeleton card (a title and three lines); no subtitle and no Create speciality until the read is in.
- **Load failed** (R2-A20a; T329, T393): StatePanel's danger `Alert` with `.alert-row`, "Could not load the specialities. Nothing has changed. Try again, or come back in a few minutes." and an outline small **Try again** (`refresh-cw`). The cause goes to the log, never the exception's text. No Create speciality, no empty state under it. A failed read is not College unavailable.

## Narrow widths

- **From 1151px** (`@media (min-width: 1151px)`, a2878763 and 7d4c4490): the Description column takes 40% (`.college-tree-page .clinic-table th.col-wrap--wide`) and the Default scale 24% (`th.col-wrap--scale`); Status and the actions are `.col-fit` (1%, no wrap), so the room goes to the text. At 1280 (replay 1.12 and 6.12, re-taken on 7d4c4490): each description on one line in 415px, "CPSA Paediatric Entrustment Scale v11.1" on two lines in 249px, Status 76px holding "Active", the actions 78px holding Edit; each row 81px high. Until a2878763 the description ran one word a line at `.col-wrap`'s 14% beside an auto actions column 200px wider than its Edit, and until 7d4c4490 the Default scale ran over three lines beside a 218px Status.
- **From 1025 to 1150px** the table keeps the shared widths: `.col-wrap` 14% (at least 7rem on `.people-page`), `.col-wrap--wide` 20%.
- **At 1024px and below every section's table stacks** (flow 16's shared part, `.college-tree-page .clinic-table--stack-wide`; DESIGN.md § Table system): a block per sub-speciality, its name first, then "Description:", "Default scale:", "Status:" and Edit, 44px. The build's sweep (900 to 1100px) and the replay (1.12, 6.12 at 1280, 1100, 1025 and 1000) found every table as wide as its container.
- **A speciality's head** wraps (`.form-section-head`, centred on the tree by `.college-tree-page .form-section-head`): Edit and Create sub-speciality go under the name when they do not fit. At 390 with "Paediatrics" they sit beside the h2 (states pass 1, `specialities-list--narrow`), as R3-A02-390 draws. Below 641px `.people-page .btn` makes them, and every button on the page, 44px.
- **A speciality's line** breaks anywhere (`.college-tree-page .user-section > p`), so a 2,000-character description wraps rather than widen the page (R3-A22).
- **The header**: from 1024px Create speciality stays on the h1's row and the subtitle wraps under the h1 (Q20, `.header-container`); below it the header wraps.

## Contrast

No new colour token. A speciality's line and "No sub-specialities yet. …" in `muted-text`, 4.86:1 on the page's ground (a section is not a card); a stacked cell's label in `muted-text`, 5.09:1 on the table's surface.

## Known gaps

- **The College admin's trail while the tree reads, and on a failed read, is Home › Specialities › Specialities**: the page's own crumb is the College's name (`CollegeCrumb`), and until the tree is read it falls back to the h1. Inferred from the code (`PageHeader` draws `CurrentCrumb ?? Title`); the states' loading and load-error captures would show it.
- **The old Sub-specialities address on a failed read**: its Try again focuses nothing once it reads (its `StatePanel` has no `FocusAfterRetry`), and its words and h1 ("Could not load the specialities.", "Specialities") speak of the tree, though it read one speciality. No step reaches it.
- **Reactivating a speciality or a sub-speciality returns here with only "Speciality saved." or "Sub-speciality saved."** (T394's states pass 1, unfiled): the tree's token says saved, never reactivated, where the College's record says "reactivated".
- A sub-speciality whose name another of the College shares ("‹name› (‹speciality›)", N4) is drawn by no board, and no step reaches it.
