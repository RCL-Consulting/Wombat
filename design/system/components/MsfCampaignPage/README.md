# MsfCampaignPage

A campaign's page (`/msf/campaigns/{id}`, `Pages/MultiSourceFeedback/CampaignEdit.razor`; its words in `MsfCampaignText.cs`): **the campaign as it is now, offering only what its state allows**: the status card, a draft's addresses, and About this campaign. Flow 10 (T369, 2026-10-19, 68327c9d; boards R3-K1 to K22; round 3 F7, F11, K1–K22, M2, M4, M8, M10, S3, S4, S13, S14; T199, T206, T217, T225, T234, T247, T251, T270, T284, T329; build review D6, D7, R7) rebuilt it. Until then it also held the create form, its state was a details list with a Status row, a draft's invitees were a table that scrolled at 390, an add said "Invitee added: peer-1@… (Peer doctor)", and Open said links "have been emailed".

## Who reads it

Coordinator and Administrator, each only a campaign in their scope; an id that names no campaign this caller runs, or one about the caller, is "Campaign unavailable" (T113, T269). Owned by MSF campaigns (NavMenu): Home › MSF campaigns › "MSF campaign for Lerato Molefe". Someone who holds Trainee, or a Coordinator at no institution, gets the standing warning (`#msf-runs-no-campaigns`) above the unavailable card.

## Withdraw and Remove (ConfirmDialog, DangerAction)

Withdraw: worded as the list's (MsfCampaigns), Keep the campaign / Withdraw campaign; for one closed and under review it says its report will never be released and mentions no links or addresses, which closing already removed (T199). Remove: "Remove this invitee?" · "Remove grace.mokwena@kgk.wombat.local (Nurse) from this campaign? They hold no working link, and will not be sent one when the campaign opens. While the campaign is a draft, they can be added again." · **Keep the invitee** · Remove invitee. Its result: "grace.mokwena@kgk.wombat.local (Nurse) has been removed from this campaign, and will not be emailed a link when it opens."

## Busy and refused (T234, T329)

No action is natively disabled: while one runs its button says so with `aria-disabled` ("Opening campaign…", "Resending links…") and a second press of any sends nothing. A handler's own refusal (`MsfRefusedException`) shows its words; anything else is "Something went wrong. Nothing has changed. Try again, or come back in a few minutes." and goes to the log (build review R1, R2). The page is read again after every action, done or refused.

## States (K16–K18)

- **Loading**: the header at once ("MSF campaign"), two skeleton cards, said once ("Loading the campaign."), no action.
- **Load failed**: fixed words with Try again (StatePanel), never the exception's text.
- **Unavailable** (K16): "Campaign unavailable" (h1 and tab), the empty card "The selected campaign could not be loaded." with an outline **Go to MSF campaigns**.

## Narrow widths

Below 641px every button on the page is 44px; the status card's actions stack the card's width, the primary on top; the add form's row stacks at 900px and below (S14), its button full width below 641px; the counts and the Links part go one under the other when two columns do not fit; the dialogs' buttons stack, the action on top (M10). Nothing scrolls sideways at 390.

## Contrast

No new colour token. The stripes and badges as ActivityStatus's and Badge's; the caption and an address's group (`muted-text`) 5.09:1; counts 12.63:1; the warning's words 11.89:1 on `warning-bg`.

## Anonymity

Once opened the page lists nobody: counts by group, never an address, never which invitee responded; Links says how many, never whose. Closing removes every address. The draft's address list is the one place addresses show, to whoever runs the campaign, while no invitee holds a working link or has answered (T247 review).
