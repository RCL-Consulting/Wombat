# TraineeProfile

A trainee's programme (`/admin/trainees/edit?id=`, `Pages/Admin/Trainees/TraineeProfileEdit.razor`; its words `TraineeProfileWords.cs`, the refusals' `AdmissionText.cs`; the reads `GetTraineeProfileById.cs`, `GetTakeBackForecast.cs`; the ends `CompleteTraineeProfile.cs`, `DeactivateTraineeProfile.cs`, each returning `ProgrammeEndResult(TakenBack)`; the move's words `TraineeMoveWords.cs`, its read `TraineeCurriculumChoices.cs`, its save `UpdateTraineeProfile.cs`): **the programme's details, and where it ends**. Flow 13 (T379, 2026-10-27, 5cc10c66; boards R3-P01 to P25; T2, Q13, Q21, Q24, M2, F5, F8, F10, N6, N13, N14, S3, S4, S19, A5; build review B2, P4) rebuilt it in flow 12's column of sections. Until then it was "Trainee profile", ended a programme with two buttons that asked nothing and named no day, refused a day after the dialog, printed its read's exception (T329) and offered Back to trainees. **Flow 15** (T392, 2026-11-02, c931d56f; boards R2-D01 to D07b, R3-D03; Q8, Q20, M1, M2, S1, decision 6; build review P2) added **the move**: the line under Curriculum that says what a save does with the version chosen, "Save profile and move…" and its dialog, and the move's refusal under the picker (T304's remainder: until then a move saved with nothing asked).

## Who reads it

Administrator and Institutional admin (`AdministratorOrInstitutionalAdmin`), each in their scope; an id outside it is the Unavailable page, as an unknown one is (S4). Someone who also holds Trainee reads nothing (T278, T290): the header and one standing info alert, `#trainee-profile-trainee-note`, asked before any read. Reached from Trainees' Edit (a running programme) or View (an ended one) (Trainees); the trail is Home › Trainees › ‹name›'s programme.

## The end (Q21, Q24, M2, S3, S19)

1. **The press checks the day first**, by the rule both commands hold it to (`TraineeProfile.EndDayRefusal`), on the South African today read at the press (B2): none typed, "Choose the last day."; before the start, "The last day cannot be before the programme start date (2023-01-15)."; after today, "The last day cannot be after today (2026-10-27).". Refused, **no dialog opens**: the refusal is a `div.validation-message[role="alert"]#programme-end-refusal` under the field, named first by its `aria-describedby`, the input `aria-invalid`, the focus in it, the typed day kept.
2. **Otherwise the dialog opens** with what the end would take back for that day, read as it opens (`GetTakeBackForecastQuery`, the commands' own count): Mark complete's confirms with the primary, Deactivate's with the filled danger button (ConfirmDialog, "Flow 13's two").
3. **The result** sits at the top of the column, above the summary, in an `ActionResult` that takes the focus, its first sentence bold, the page now the ended record: "**Trainee marked complete.** The Trainee role has been removed and a graduation email sent. 1 activity observed after that day no longer counts." / "**Trainee profile deactivated.** The last day in the programme is recorded as 2026-10-25, and the Trainee role has been removed." (the count only when above zero, from the command's `TakenBack`).
4. **The command's own refusal** at confirm shows under the field as the press-time one does (P07); **any other failure** is fixed words at the section's top, focused: "**The programme could not be ended just now.** Nothing has changed. Try again, or come back in a few minutes."

## The move (flow 15: T392, Q8, Q20, M1, M2)

- **The line under the picker** is a `p.submit-check#curriculum-id-line` (flow 03's check line: `info-bg`, a 4px `secondary-color` edge), standing content and no live region, named in the select's `aria-describedby` after any refusal. It says what a save does with the version chosen (`TraineeMoveWords`), naming the registrar each time, never a pronoun (M1):
  - the active adoption, unchanged: "The version your institution has adopted for Paediatrics / Paediatrics." (R2-D05);
  - kept on a version the institution has superseded: "Anele Dlamini is on Paediatric EPA Curriculum (11.1), which your institution superseded on 2026-11-02. Keeping it keeps Anele Dlamini on 11.1." (R2-D01; with no later adoption to date it, "… which is not the version your institution has adopted. …"); Save profile asks nothing;
  - a move chosen: "When you save, Sipho Ndlovu moves to Paediatric EPA Curriculum (11.2). Sipho Ndlovu's completed activities are counted again against 11.2 in the same save, so Sipho Ndlovu's progress page reads 11.2's targets at once. Sipho Ndlovu cannot be moved back to 11.1 while 11.2 is the version your institution has adopted." (R2-D02; M2: no way back only while the new version is adopted).
- **The submit asks first** while the picker differs from the pin (the button, or Enter in any field): after the page's own checks, a `ConfirmDialog` (action mode, so Cancel takes the focus) titled "Move Sipho Ndlovu to Paediatric EPA Curriculum (11.2)?", its body two paragraphs, "Sipho Ndlovu's completed activities are counted again against 11.2 as the profile saves, and from then on they credit 11.2's items." and the no-way-back sentence; its confirm **Move and save**, the primary (a move is not a loss). No count is read before it (Q20). Cancel or Escape closes it with nothing saved and every field as typed, the focus back on "Save profile and move…".
- **Moved**: the section's result, focused, the command's message with its first sentence bold: "**Trainee profile saved.** 1 completion was checked against 11.2, and 1 counts towards it." (T304; "… has no completions to count against 11.2." for none). The picker is read again (P2: apart from the save, so a failed re-read never turns a saved move into "could not be saved"), and offers 11.2 alone under the active-adoption line.
- **Refused** (R2-D06): a move into a version the institution no longer has adopted (a stale page) is the command's `FieldRefusalException` on `CurriculumId` (`FieldRefusal.Of`), shown under the picker, "A trainee can be moved only into the curriculum version this institution has adopted for their discipline, so nothing was saved.", as `div.validation-message[role="alert"]#curriculum-id-refusal`, first in the select's `aria-describedby`, the select `aria-invalid` and focused; the picker is read again and set back to the pin when the chosen version is no longer offered, every other field as typed.

## Saving the details (P4)

The page's own checks sit under their field (Programme start date's a `p.validation-message`; Curriculum's, since flow 15, the `div.validation-message[role="alert"]#curriculum-id-refusal` the move's refusal uses), the field `aria-invalid` and described by it, the first focused; a changed field clears its refusal: "Choose a curriculum.", "Enter the programme start." (Admit's words). No `ValidationSummary`. The command's own refusal (a validation, scope or state refusal) is the section's result above the form, in its words, none bold, focused. Saved, the result is above the form in its section, focused, the command's message with its first sentence bold (a move, or a change of start, says what its replay checked, T304). Failed: "The profile could not be saved just now. Nothing has changed. Try again, or come back in a few minutes."

## States

- **Loading**: `p.loading-line[role="status"]` "Loading this trainee profile." over three skeleton cards, `aria-busy` on the column.
- **Load failed**: "Could not load this trainee profile. Nothing has changed. Try again, or come back in a few minutes." with Try again (StatePanel), which focuses the h1.
- **Profile unavailable** (S4): the empty card "This trainee profile does not exist, or you cannot open it." and **Go to Trainees** (`btn-primary`, `arrow-right`).
