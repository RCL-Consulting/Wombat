# VerifyExport

Verify portfolio export (`/portfolio/verify`, `Pages/Portfolio/VerifyExport.razor`; its words `VerifyExportWords.cs`; its read `Wombat.Application/Features/Reporting/VerifyExport.cs`, `VerifyExportQuery` and its typed `VerifyExportRefusal`): **whoever holds a portfolio PDF checks here whether Wombat produced it, signed in or not.** Flow 13 (T379, 2026-10-27, 5cc10c66; boards R3-V01 to V10; F9, S10, S20, S21, N4, N15, Q6) rebuilt it. Until then it told a verifier that a genuine PDF was not Wombat's once its export was 90 days old or when its hash was typed in upper case (T313), printed its answer in UTC (T325), and used an `h3` and an inline style (T280).

## Who reads it

Everyone: `[AllowAnonymous]`, **static HTML for every visitor** (`[ExcludeFromInteractiveRouting]`), in the shell for a signed-in person and under the signed-out bar for anyone else. The form is a plain GET (`/portfolio/verify?hash=…&check=1`), so it needs no circuit; the check is made as the page renders. It is linked from the export's result (ExportPortfolio) and the former trainee's Home card (FormerTraineeHome), each a plain `<a href>`, a full navigation.

## The page

The header "Verify portfolio export", "Check whether a PDF was produced by Wombat, without signing in.", no trail, no action. Then `.verify-page`, a flex column 24px apart at most 44rem: the answer when there is one, then `form.form-container` (`method="get"`):

- One `.form-group`: the label **Content hash (SHA-256) \*** (the `*` hidden, "required" said); `input#hash.form-control.hash-value` (monospace 0.875rem, breaking anywhere; `required`, `autocomplete="off"`, `spellcheck="false"`), what was typed kept; the refusal when there is one; `p.field-help#hash-h` "The file's SHA-256: 64 hexadecimal digits, computed on your computer from the PDF you were sent. Upper or lower case, and spaces or line breaks in it, are accepted."; then `ul.cmd-list#hash-cmds` (Q6, muted 0.9rem, one tool a line, each command a `code` on `header-bg`): "Windows: `certutil -hashfile <file> SHA256`, or in PowerShell `Get-FileHash <file>`", "macOS: `shasum -a 256 <file>`", "Linux: `sha256sum <file>`".
- `.form-actions`: **Verify** (`btn-primary`, `shield-check`, `name="check" value="1"`), so the page can tell a press from a link.

The query drops every whitespace character (U+200B and U+FEFF too) and the case before it looks, so Get-FileHash's upper case and certutil's spaced form verify (T313, S10).

## The answer (F9, S20, S21)

An `ActionResult` above the form holding one Alert, its first sentence bold. **After a press of Verify** it takes the focus as the page loads (`FocusOnLoad`, `autofocus`); **from a link** (`?hash=` alone) it leaves the focus alone (V10).

- **Verified** (success, `role="status"`): "**Export verified.** Wombat generated this PDF: its content is byte for byte an export Wombat recorded." Then `dl.detail-list` (8px under it; its value column `minmax(0, 1fr)`, each `dd` breaking anywhere): **First generated** "2026-10-27 23:20 SAST"; **Exported** "Once" or "3 times, the last on 2026-10-27 23:22 SAST"; **Trainee** and **First exported by**, Wombat user ids in `.hash-value`; **Filter** "2023-01-15 to 2026-10-27" ("From …", "Up to …"; only when the export was filtered); **File** "portfolio-9ac3aea9a795.pdf". Then `p.verify-ids-note` (muted 0.875rem): "People are shown by their Wombat user ids, never by name. The institution that issued the portfolio can say whose they are." Times are SAST, `yyyy-MM-dd HH:mm`, never UTC (T325).
- **Not found** (danger, `role="alert"`): "**No matching export found.** This hash does not correspond to any PDF generated by this system. One changed byte changes the whole hash: check that it was computed from the file you were sent."
- **The check failed** (danger): "**The export could not be checked just now.** Try again in a few minutes."; the cause to the log, never the error page (T265).

## What is not a hash (S10, N4, N15)

Refused under the field, `div.validation-message#hash-e`, from the query's typed reason: only spaces, "Enter the content hash to check."; a character that is no hexadecimal digit, named: "This is not a SHA-256 hash: “z” is not a hexadecimal digit (0 to 9, a to f)."; the 12 digits a file's name carries: "This is not a SHA-256 hash: it has 12 characters, and a SHA-256 hash has 64 hexadecimal digits (0 to 9, a to f). The 12 in a file's name are only its start."; any other length: "… it has 63 characters, and …". The input is then `aria-invalid` and `.input-validation-error`, keeps what was typed, is described by the refusal first (`aria-describedby="hash-e hash-h hash-cmds"`) and carries `autofocus` after a press, so a static page puts the focus in it with no script. No answer is drawn.

## Narrow widths

Below 641px, inside `.verify-page`: the details list is one column, each term over its value; the field and Verify are 44px and Verify is the row's width; the field is 1rem, so iOS does not zoom the page on focus (P8), while the answer's ids keep `.hash-value`'s 0.875rem. A 64-digit hash stays on one line in its field and wraps in the answer. Measured on the replay (wombat_scenario_t379, signed out: the form, a refusal, verified and not found) at 1280 and 390: no sideways scroll, every control 44px at 390.
