# DataRightsRequest

A data rights request (`/admin/data-rights/{Id}`, `Pages/Admin/DataRights/RequestDetail.razor`; its words `DataRightsRequestWords.cs` (with `DataRightsMessage`, a lead and the rest), the shared ones `Components/Shared/DataRightsWords.cs`, `DataRightsLabels`, `DataRightsRecords`, `DataRightsRefusalSentences`; the reads `GetDataRightsRequestByIdQuery`, `GetErasureForecastQuery`; the commands `ApproveDataRightsRequestCommand` (its `ErasureOutcome` for an erasure), `RejectDataRightsRequestCommand`, `ApplyRectificationCommand`, `CompleteRectificationRequestCommand`; refusals by `DataRightsRefusedException.Reason`): **one request: what was asked, what was decided, the correction an approved Rectification carries, and the decision itself, an erasure through its dialog.** Flow 14 (T387, 2026-10-30, e8eea1d2; boards R3-D01 to D22, C01 to C11, K01, U01, U02; Q3, Q12, Q13, Q17, Q19, Q26, F1, F3, F5, F6, F9, M2, N2, N8, N14, N17, N18, S2, S6, S8, S11, S13, S14, S21; build review S1, P4 to P6, P10, P11) rebuilt it as a column of sections. Until then it was a details grid that printed enum names and exception text, approved an erasure with no confirmation, and could never apply or complete an approved correction (T316).

## Who reads it

Administrator and Coordinator (`[Authorize(Roles = "Administrator,Coordinator")]`), each in their scope. Reached from the queue's Review (DataRightsRequests). The trail is Home › Data rights requests › the request's id, from the first render (`CurrentCrumb`, N14; Breadcrumbs); the menu lights Data rights requests.

## States

- **Loading**: `p.loading-line[role="status"]` "Loading the request." over two skeleton cards; h1 "Data rights request", the crumb already the id.
- **Load failed**: "Could not load this request. Nothing has changed. Try again, or come back in a few minutes." with Try again (StatePanel), which focuses the h1 once it reads.
- **Request unavailable**: above.

## Narrow widths

Below 641px `.user-page`'s rules hold: every control 44px, a described radio's label a 44px row, the form actions stacked with the primary on top (Decide: Approve, or Approve and erase…, over Reject; Reject first in the DOM, so first in the tab order), each the row's width; Value before and Value after one column (`.correction-values`); the dialog's buttons stacked with Erase and approve on top. Corrections recorded stacks from 900px, its record the block's first line with no rule under it. The inline "Open ‹name›'s programme" link in the programme start's help is 20px at 390, the inline-link exemption of WCAG 2.5.8 (review P10), as flow 13's Verify link is. Measured on the replay at 390, 768, 900 and 1280: nothing sideways.
