What each rung means ShowHide
1
Not entrusted to act. The supervisor performs the activity and thinks aloud; the trainee prepares, observes actively, and may not enact or document decisions.
2
Direct supervision. Supervisor present throughout, sharing the task or watching silently, ready to step in. The plan may be entirely the trainee's, but nothing is acted on without sign-off.
3a
Indirect supervision, decisions checked. Supervisor on site or immediately available and reviews findings, working diagnosis and plan before they are enacted. Trainee may act alone on routine assessment, investigation and supportive care within protocol.
3b
Indirect supervision, reviewed on the trainee’s initiative. Supervisor available on request; the trainee decides what needs discussion and shows reliable help-seeking. Full management of the activity, supervisor informed rather than consulted.
4
Unsupervised practice. The trainee carries the responsibility for the activity.
5
Supervises others. The trainee carries the responsibility and can supervise juniors in the activity.
