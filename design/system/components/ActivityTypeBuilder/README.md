# ActivityTypeBuilder

The activity type builder (`/admin/activity-types/new` and `/admin/activity-types/{id}`, `Pages/Admin/ActivityTypes/ActivityTypeEdit.razor` with `ActivityTypeEdit.razor.cs`; its words `ActivityTypeEditWords.cs` and `ActivityTypeWords.cs`; refusals `BuilderRefusal.cs`; the read `GetActivityTypeEditorQuery`, `ActivityTypeEditorDto`; the commands `SaveActivityTypeDraftCommand`, `PublishActivityTypeDraftCommand`, `DiscardActivityTypeDraftCommand`): **one activity type's details, form, workflow and credit rules as a tabs pattern, saved as a draft and published as a version.** This record holds the page's frame: the header, the result region, the version strip, the tabs, Details, Publish, the three questions and the page's states. The Form tab is ActivityTypeOutline; the Workflow and Credit tabs' read in words and the versions table are ActivityTypeReadouts. Flow 17 (T398, 2026-11-10, 84bcffeb, with c9742a18: a `seed:` scale key read by its scale's name, a tab's count keeping its space; boards R3-B07, C01 to C23, D01 to D05, E01 to E17 and round 2's standing boards; Q1 to Q20, S1 to S28, T300, T334, the operator's F1) rebuilt it. Until then it had pill tabs Metadata, Form, Workflow and Credit with no tab semantics, a Back to list button, the field editor below the whole list, the JSON alone, a refusal as the server's message in one alert, and Publish beside Save draft with no question.

## Who reads it

The Administrator, the College admin and the Institutional admin (`[Authorize(Policy = "NationalCatalogueAccess")]`), each a **writer** where the editor's `CanWrite` holds (the rule the commands refuse by: the caller's scope admits the type and it is not system-managed, T300, T334) and a **reader** elsewhere. The trail is the way back ("Home › Activity types › ‹name›"; no Back to list); NavMenu's Activity types is lit.

## The header

- **Titles** (the h1, the tab and the last crumb): "New activity type"; a writer's "Edit ‹name›"; a reader's the name alone, "Mini-CEX (Paediatrics)"; "Activity type" while loading or after a failed read; "Activity type unavailable". The stored name, never the typed one.
- **The subtitle** "What a registrar fills in, who moves it on, and what it counts towards." and under it **the owner line** (`span.builder-owner-line`, a block line of its own inside the subtitle, 0.875rem; Q12): the owner then the scope, "Kgosi Kgari Teaching Hospital · Institution", "CPSA · Speciality · Paediatrics" (the College by its short code), "The platform · Global".
- **A writer's actions**: **Save draft** (`btn-primary`, `#builder-save`), **Publish** (`btn-success`, `#builder-publish`) and, with a saved draft, **Discard draft…** (`btn-outline`, `aria-haspopup="dialog"`). Without a draft Publish is unavailable, not disabled (S5): `aria-disabled="true"`, inert, still in the tab order, with "Save a draft to publish." beside it as text (`p.muted.text-sm#publish-reason`, its `aria-describedby`). While one action runs the other two are natively disabled and the one pressed is `aria-disabled` (T234). Save draft and Publish carry `aria-haspopup="dialog"` while Active is newly unticked on a published type (they ask first). A reader is offered none of them; on a system-managed type that holds a draft from before T334, Discard draft… alone, to whoever its scope admits (F1).

## Under the header, above the tabs

1. **One result region** (`ActionResult`) at every width: a result ("Draft saved.", "Published version 1.", "Draft discarded.", with a tail such as "KGK Teaching Session Log is inactive: registrars are no longer offered it." or "Nothing was published."; a `success` Alert, its lead bold), a failure (a danger Alert, "**The draft could not be saved.** Nothing has changed. Try again, or come back in a few minutes.", "Version 2 could not be published.", "The draft could not be discarded."), the scope guard's sentence (a danger Alert, no place), or the refusal summary (below). It takes the focus once the action has answered. A result stays until the next save, publish or discard; a tab press is none of them (E02b). A first Save draft on New lands on the type's own address with "Draft saved." focused (`?created`, taken out, T291 item 3).
2. **A reader's standing notice**, an info Alert with no role: T300's "Set by the College that owns Paediatrics. You can read this activity type here, but not change it." ("Set by Kgosi Kgari Teaching Hospital. …", "Set by a global administrator. …"), or on a system-managed type the release's "Written by the MSF release. Its form changes only with a release of Wombat. Nobody is offered it when filing an activity."
3. **The version strip**, `dl.detail-list.builder-strip` (no `aria-label`): Published "Version 1, 2026-11-10, Nolwazi Mbatha" or "Not published"; Draft "Saved 2026-11-10 by Nolwazi Mbatha" or "None". The seeder is "Wombat's catalogue"; a reader outside the type's scope reads no person ("Version 1, 2026-11-10", "Saved 2026-11-10").
4. On a seed-owned type with a draft, to a writer: "While this draft is saved, updates from Wombat's catalogue stop reaching this type, until you publish or discard it."

## The tabs (Q1, T280)

`div.tab-bar[role="tablist"][aria-label="Parts of this activity type"]` stands on the page ground above the card; each tab a `button.tab-bar-tab[role="tab"]` (`#tab-details`, `#tab-form`, …) with `aria-selected`, a roving `tabindex` (0 on the selected) and `aria-controls` on the selected only; the card is the tabpanel (`role="tabpanel"`, `#panel-‹tab›`, `aria-labelledby` its tab, `tabindex="0"` only where it holds no control). A writer has five tabs, **Details, Form, Workflow, Credit, Publish**; a reader four (no Publish). A new type opens on Details, an existing one on Form; a save keeps the tab; Publish lands on the tab it was pressed from (Q20). Left, Right, Home and End move the focus (manual activation: Enter or Space selects). **A tab with refusals carries its count in its name** (M2, R1): `Form <span class="badge badge-declined">1<span> problem</span></span>`, "Form 1 problem", "Workflow 2 problems". See TabBar for the look and the widths.

## Details

- **A writer's**: `section.detail-card.user-section[role="tabpanel"]`, h2 "Details", then Q14's line "Saved with Save draft, not with Publish: a change here reaches everyone when you save." and "* marks a field you must fill in." (`RequiredMark`). **Key*** (`input.font-mono`, help "Fixed once published."; once published a read-out, `kgk_teaching_log` over "Fixed: version 1 is published."), **Name***, **Scope** (a read-out "Institution · Kgosi Kgari Teaching Hospital" over "The one scope you can write in." where one scope at one target is offered; else a required `fieldset.choice-group[role="radiogroup"]` of exactly the scopes the caller may write in, Speciality and Sub-speciality for the College, and the target under it: "None: a Global type belongs to no institution or speciality.", a read-out for one target, a required select for several), **This tool is** ("Not a WBA instrument" and the College's twelve instruments, with its help), **Description** with its counter "0 of 2,000 characters" (`aria-describedby`), **Active** with "An inactive type is not offered to anyone filing an activity. Activities already filed are not changed."
- **A reader's**: a `dl.detail-list` (Key in `<code>`, Name, Set by, This tool is, Description or "None", Status), then the versions (ActivityTypeReadouts' VersionsTable, its heading an h2), the panel focusable itself.

## Publish (a writer's tab)

h2 "Publish". With no draft: "There is no draft to publish: version 1 is what registrars file. Save a draft to publish." or "Nothing is published yet. Save a draft to publish." With one, read from the **saved** draft (S6): "Save draft to see what publishing changes." first while edits are unsaved; on a first publish h3 "What you are publishing", a `dl` (Form "1 section, 6 fields (5 required)", Workflow "From Draft to Logged or Cancelled; 2 moves", Credit "Credits nothing") and "Nothing to compare: this is the first version."; else h3 "What publishing this draft changes", "Nothing on the form changes for activities already filed." or the warnings as a list ("Field 'Audience' will be removed.") and the pinned line "Activities already filed stay on version 1, with Audience as they filed it. Only activities filed after the publish use version 2."; on a seed-owned type "Once you publish it, updates from Wombat's catalogue no longer reach this type." Then the versions (h3) and the tab's own **Publish version 2** (`btn-success`, unavailable as the header's is, with "Save a draft to publish." under it).

## The questions (ConfirmDialog)

- **Publish** (Q16, S7): Publish first saves the edits since the draft was saved, then asks only when the saved draft has warnings: "Publish version 2 of KGK Teaching Session Log?", "Publishing makes this draft version 2. It changes:", the warnings, the pinned line; Cancel and **Publish version 2**, a **primary** (not a danger) button. Cancel or Escape: nothing published ("Draft saved. Nothing was published." where it saved first), the focus back on the Publish pressed.
- **Deactivate** (Q15, Q17): Active newly unticked on a published type asks at Save draft, and at Publish before anything else: "Deactivate KGK Teaching Session Log?", "Registrars are no longer offered it when filing. Activities already filed are not changed." and "You can reactivate it on this page."; Cancel and Deactivate (danger). At Publish the publish question then opens alone, one dialog at a time. Ticking Active back asks nothing ("… is active again: registrars are offered it.").
- **Discard draft** (Q14, F1): "Discard the draft of KGK Teaching Session Log?", for a published type "Its form, workflow and credit rules go back to version 1, which stays published. Its details (name, description, scope, This tool is, Active) were saved as you changed them, and stay.", never published "It has never been published. Its form, workflow and credit rules go back to the starting draft; its details stay."; Cancel and **Discard draft** (danger).
- Each opens on Cancel; `ReturnFocusId` gives the focus back to the trigger.

## Refusals and failures (Q6, P1, P2)

**The builder tells a refusal from a failure itself and never calls `RefusalText.Of`** (T399 is the other pages). A refusal (the three parse exceptions and their aggregate, a field refusal, the Details validator's words) is a `RefusalSummary` at the top of the result region, "**Nothing was saved.** Everything you typed is kept." ("Nothing was published." for a refused publish), every tab's problems at once (P2), one line a place prefixed by its place: "Details:", "Form:", "Form, section ‹title›:", "Workflow, move ‹label›:", "Workflow:", "Credit:". Each line is a link whose press (`LineAction`) switches the tab, opens the row's editor in place and focuses its key, the move's JSON or the Details control. The words stand again at the place (`p.validation-message`, the control `aria-invalid`, the words first in its `aria-describedby`), and each tab's count is in its name. What the page can check before sending (an empty or over-long Key or Name, "Enter a key.", "A name is at most 200 characters."; an empty section key or field key) refuses the same way with nothing sent; the rest is the server's, worded at its place by the page ("Key 'topic' is also the key of Audience. Give each field its own key.", "This section has no fields. Add a field, or delete the section.", "Move Cancel cannot be read: 'topic' (Topic) is not a User field, so it names nobody.") while the summary's line keeps the server's words ("Form: Field key 'topic' is used by 2 fields ('Topic' in 'Teaching session', 'Audience' in 'Teaching session'); give each field its own key."). The scope guard's sentence ("You do not have permission to modify activity types in that speciality.") is a danger Alert in the region with no place. Anything else is the fixed failure, logged, never the exception's text (T329, T393).

## States

- **Loading** (R2-B10): h1 "Activity type", no subtitle or action; "Loading this activity type." over a skeleton card; `aria-busy` on the column.
- **Load failed** (R2-B11): "Could not load this activity type. Nothing has changed. Try again, or come back in a few minutes." with Try again; no tabs. Try again focuses the h1.
- **Unavailable** (R2-B09, N3): an unknown id, or one the caller may not read: h1 "Activity type unavailable", "This activity type does not exist, or you cannot open it." and **Go to Activity types** (primary) in a `.detail-card--empty`, never Access denied, no id shown; the h1 is the last crumb.
- **No scope** (R2-C08-new): `/new` for a caller admitted but with no scope to create in (a College with no speciality): "New activity type" over the standing notice "You have no scope to create an activity type in. An institution's administrators create their institution's types, and a College's administrators the types of its specialities and sub-specialities.", no tabs and no Save draft.

## Narrow widths

The page is `.user-page.activity-type-page`, a size container (`builder-page`): the tabs go by the column's width, not the viewport's (TabBar). The header's actions stay beside the title from 1024px (PageHeader); below 641px they take the row, 44px. Details' `form-grid` falls to one field a row; the lone Active checkbox below 641px is the shared 44px row with a 20px box. The sweep (900 to 1150px by 25, 1115, 1116, and 641 to 768 with counts standing) found nothing wider than its box and the page never scrolled sideways.

## Contrast

No new colour token. The owner line and the strip's terms `muted-text` (5.09:1 on the surface, 4.83:1 on the page ground for the Publish reason); the result and refusal words `text-color` on their tints (11.81:1 on `danger-bg`); `.btn-success`'s words `on-fill` 5.88:1.

## Known gaps

- **The pinned line's verb is singular for several fields**: "Activities already filed stay on version 1, with Audience and Topic as they filed it." (`ActivityTypeEditWords.PinnedLine`; "them" for two or more). Found re-syncing, not fixed.
- **The empty result region keeps a row** (inferred from app.css): the builder's column is `.user-page`, a flex column 24px apart, and the region is its first child with no `.activity-type-page .action-result:empty` rule (as the curriculum's, the EPA's and the speciality's pages have), so with no result the strip or the notice stands 24px lower than the page's other first blocks.
- **Other pages still print an exception's text as a refusal** (T399, proposed): only the builder and the list tell a refusal from a failure.
- A save that only unticks Active stages a draft though the form, workflow and credit rules are as published, so Discard draft… and Publish version 2 appear with "Nothing on the form changes for activities already filed." (by design: `SaveDraft` stages whenever it is called; the build review's N6).
- Not seen in the replay: whether a result still reads after a tab press (Step 1.30 re-opened the page; DESIGN.md says it stays).
