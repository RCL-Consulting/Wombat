# UserPage

One person's page (`/admin/users/{UserId}`, `Pages/Admin/Users/UserDetail.razor`; its words in `UserPageText.cs` and `UserPageWords.cs`, the rules in `Wombat.Application/Features/Users/UserAdministrationRules.cs`): **one column of sections under a who line**, where an administrator gives and takes a person's roles, resets a password, removes an institutional sign-in, and locks the account out or reactivates it. Assessor training is a section of its own, written by its own component (AssessorTraining). Flow 12 (T376, 2026-10-23, e53d4ae7; boards R3-P01 to P36; Q1, Q5, Q9, Q10, Q13, Q17, F2, F3, F6, F7, F11, M2, M8 to M10, S2, S3, S6, S13, S19, S22, S23; T264's users half, T278, T323) rebuilt it. Until then it was a details grid with a card of role checkboxes, a scope per role, a Pending invitations card, a red Lock out that asked nothing, the role keys run into their notes ("PendingTraineeSystem-managed", T323), and "Back to users" in the header.

## Who reads it

Administrator and Institutional admin, each in their scope; an id outside it is "User unavailable". Someone who also holds Trainee reads nobody: the h1 "User" and the standing note `#user-trainee-note` (T278, S8). On **the caller's own page** the standing info note `#user-own-account-note` says "This is your own account, so you cannot change its roles, lockout or password here. Another administrator can change your roles or lockout. Change your password from your account." (the link to `/account/change-password`), the subtitle is "Your own account: your roles and how you sign in.", and there is no Remove…, no Add a role…, no Password or Lockout part; her own institutional sign-in is flow 02's My account.

## The page

- **The header**: the person's name as h1 and tab ("User" before the read, on a failure and when not found), "Roles, sign-in and lockout for one person at ‹institution›, with assessor training for an Assessor." (the Administrator's without "at ‹institution›"). No header action. **The trail** is Home › Users › ‹name›, and **its Users crumb returns to the filtered Users page it was opened from** (`?back=`, read only through `PeopleLinks.SafeUsersReturn`, which rebuilds Users' own parameters and gives `/admin/users` for anything else; `PageHeader.OwnerHref`, S6).
- **The who line**: `dl.detail-list` named "Account": Email (a break after its @), Institution (or "Global (no institution)"), Status ("Active", or the Locked out badge with the `lock` icon), Scope in `PersonScopeWords`' words. Its values break anywhere rather than widen the page (`.user-page .detail-list dd`).
- Then `section.detail-card.user-section`s, each named by its h2, their parts 16px apart: **Roles and scope**, **Assessor training** (whenever the person holds Assessor; AssessorTraining), **Sign-in and access**.
- `.user-page` is a flex column 24px apart, at most 60rem; there is no details grid (Q1).

## Results

Every action's result shows **in the part that acted** (Roles and scope, Password, Institutional sign-in, Lockout), in an `ActionResult` drawn new for each result so it takes the focus; **one result on the page at a time** (the training section's goes when the page acts, and the page's when the section acts). A success bolds only its first sentence; a refusal is a danger Alert in the same place, through `RefusalText.Of`, or "That did not go through. Nothing has changed. Try again, or come back in a few minutes."

## States

- **Loading**: `p.loading-line` "Loading this person." over three skeleton cards.
- **Load failed**: "Could not load this person. Nothing has changed. Try again, or come back in a few minutes." with Try again; a Try again that loads focuses Roles and scope's heading.
- **Not found or out of scope**: the h1 "User", the empty card "User unavailable" · "The user could not be found or is outside your scope." with **Go to Users** (`arrow-right`). The old `/admin/assessors/edit?id=…` (`AssessorProfileEdit.razor`) leads to the person's page at `?section=training`, or says the same, under Home › Users › User.

## Narrow widths

Below 641px every control on the page is 44px; a role's row stacks, its Remove… under the role at the row's width; every form's actions stack, the primary on top (`.user-page .form-actions`, `.note-panel--form .form-actions`, `column-reverse`: the DOM keeps Cancel first in the tab order), each the row's width, as Lock out… and a part's lone button; a radio's label is its 44px target (S17). The dialogs stack theirs (ConfirmDialog). Measured on the replay at 1280, 1000 and 390 (T376 step 8): no sideways scroll; Add role above Cancel, Lock out Thandi Zulu above Keep access.

## Accessibility

Each section a region named by its h2; Remove…, Lock out… and Delete… named for their row and announcing a dialog; Add a role…'s `aria-expanded` and its `aria-controls` only while the panel exists; an unavailable action stays focusable with its reason; results focused as they are drawn. No pronoun is stored, so the page says "they".

## Contrast

No new colour token. Notes and help `muted-text` 5.09:1 on the surface; the outline triggers 4.86:1; the danger buttons only in the dialogs, `on-fill` 5.95:1.
