# CurriculumRecord

A curriculum version's page (`/admin/curricula/new`, `/admin/curricula/{Id}`, `Pages/Admin/Curricula/CurriculumEdit.razor`; its words `CurriculumWords.cs`, the field refusals `CurriculumFieldMessages` in `CreateCurriculum.cs`, the reach `Components/Shared/CatalogueReachWords.cs`; the read `GetCurriculumByIdQuery` with `CurriculumDto.Reach`, `NationalItemCount` and `SubSpecialityFixed`, the reach read by `CatalogueReach`; the commands `CreateCurriculumCommand`, `UpdateCurriculumCommand` (refusing by `CurriculumSubSpecialityFixedException`) and `CloneCurriculumAsNewVersionCommand`; the addresses `CatalogueLinks`): **one version of a College's curriculum: its details, whether institutions may adopt it, how far a change reaches, and a new version made from it.** Flow 16 (T394, 2026-11-05, de4cc718; boards R3-D01 to D14, D18 to D20, R2-D15 to D17; Q2, Q4, Q5, Q7, Q9, Q19, S9, S13, S16, T2, T4, T6, T7, T8, T301, N14, N20, build-lanes decision 5) rebuilt it. Until then it was "Edit curriculum" whatever the version, a `details-grid` of two cards (Curriculum details, Clone as new version) with "Back to curricula" in the header, a box labelled "Active" that held a version back from adoption without asking or saying so, a sub-speciality that could move under a version's items and adoptions, each validator message twice (`ValidationSummary` and under the field, neither linked), no result after a create or a clone (T392's replay), and the exception's text on a failure (T393).

## Who reads it

The Administrator and the College admin (`[Authorize(Policy = "AdministratorOrCollegeAdmin")]`: every command the page sends is the College's, `CanAccessCollege`, T211). The page draws a version only where `CurriculumDto.CanEditCurriculum` is true; a version of another College, or an unknown id, is Curriculum unavailable (Q7). An Institutional admin is sent to Access denied (an institution reads a version on its items page). NavOwners lights Curricula (`book-open`: the Administrator's Catalogue group, the College admin's one ungrouped list) for it; the trail is Home › Curricula › the h1. It is reached by Create curriculum and each row's Edit on Curricula, and by "Edit this version" on the items page.

### Hold back's question (Q4, T6, N20)

Opening asks nothing; holding back asks one question naming who has adopted the version. **Hold back…** asks it, and so does **Save with the box unticked on a version stored open**. One `ConfirmDialog` (action mode, `DangerAction`, opening on Cancel; R3-D12, D14):

- Title: "Hold back Paediatric EPA Curriculum (11.1)?"
- "No institution can adopt it while it is held back."
- The reach, then whom it changes nothing for: "Adopted by 1 institution, Kgosi Kgari Teaching Hospital; 3 registrars in training on it. Holding it back changes nothing for them: Kgosi Kgari Teaching Hospital keeps it, and its registrars stay on it." ("… and its registrar stays on it." for one; several institutions "… keep it, and their registrars stay on it."; the registrars' clause left out for none; a version nobody has adopted reads "No institution has adopted this version yet." alone.)
- "You can open it again on this page."
- **Cancel**, then **Hold back** (danger). Cancel or Escape gives the focus back to the control that asked: Save after Save, Hold back… after Hold back… (`ConfirmDialog.ShowAsync(returnFocusId)`, DESIGN.md § Button system), the box left as it is, unsaved; Save asks again.
- Confirmed from Save, every edit on the form is saved. Confirmed from Hold back…, only the box changes: the version is sent as stored with the box unticked, so an edit not yet saved is neither saved nor lost.

### Results, refusals and failures

One result region at the column's top, its result focused:

- **Created** (`?created`, Q5, R3-D05): the create loads the new version's page afresh, which writes "Neonatology EPA Curriculum (1.0) created. It is held back: no institution can adopt it until you open it." once and takes the token out of the address.
- **Cloned** (`?cloned=‹the version cloned from›`, R3-D08): "Paediatric EPA Curriculum (11.2) cloned from 11.1, with its 15 national items. It is held back." ("… with its 1 national item. …", "… with no national items. …"). A stale or foreign token (not in the caller's scope, or another curriculum's) writes and focuses nothing.
- **Saved** (T7: by the box as stored): "Curriculum saved. It is open for adoption: an institution can adopt it now." or "Curriculum saved. It is held back: no institution can adopt it until you open it again." (Hold back's result too). The version is read again, so the reach, Items (n) and the read-out are as stored.
- **Q19's refusal** (R3-D20, decision 5): a save that would move the sub-speciality of a version that has gained an item or an adoption since the page was read, by its cause, items first: "Not saved. This version now holds items, so its sub-speciality can no longer change. The page has been read again." or "Not saved. Kgosi Kgari Teaching Hospital has adopted this version, so its sub-speciality can no longer change. The page has been read again." (two named "A and B have adopted …"; three or more, the first two and "1 other institution" or "‹N› other institutions"). The page reads the version again: the select becomes the read-out, every field as stored.
- **Refusals under their fields** (T324), each once as `div.validation-message[role="alert"]` (`‹input›-refusal`), the control `aria-invalid` and described by it, what was typed kept, the first refused control focused in the form's order; the page checks before it sends: "Choose a sub-speciality.", "Enter a name.", "A name is at most 200 characters.", "Enter a version.", "A version is at most 64 characters.", and under Effective to "Effective to must be on or after Effective from."; the clone's "Enter the new version." and the same length and date refusals. The command adds the duplicate, under Version (New version on the clone), only on the unique index's refusal (T272): "A curriculum with the same name and version already exists for this sub-speciality."
- **Any other failure** (T393): "The curriculum could not be created. …", "The curriculum could not be saved. …" (Hold back's too), "The new version could not be made. …", each ending "Nothing has changed. Try again, or come back in a few minutes.", the cause logged. Never the exception's text.

## Reads and focus

The page reads once per id, not when only the query changes (taking a token out reads nothing). A result, Q19's refusal and a failure take the focus in the result region once the dialog that sent them has closed; a field's refusal focuses its control; Try again focuses the h1 once it has read. A save's read-back that fails keeps the saved version with the facts it was read with (its reach, its count, its read-out).

## States

- **Loading** (R2-D16): the h1 "Curriculum" ("Create curriculum" on a create), no subtitle or header action, `p.loading-line[role="status"]` "Loading this curriculum." over a skeleton card.
- **Load failed** (R2-D17): "Could not load this curriculum. Nothing has changed. Try again, or come back in a few minutes." with Try again (StatePanel, `refresh-cw`), the cause logged.
- **Unavailable** (Q7, R2-D15): the h1 "Curriculum unavailable" and its last crumb, `.detail-card--empty` "This curriculum does not exist, or you cannot open it." with **Go to Curricula** (primary). No id is shown.

## Narrow widths

One column at every width; nothing of its own below 641px but the read-out's value, which breaks anywhere (`.curriculum-page .curriculum-read-out dd`). Below 1024px the header wraps and Items (15) goes under the title (Q20's rule holds it beside the title from 1024px); below 641px it takes the row at 44px (M9). Below 641px `.user-page` makes every input, select and button 44px and stacks each form's actions with the primary on top, each the row's width (`column-reverse`: Save, then Hold back…, then Cancel on screen; Cancel stays first in the tab order); the Open for adoption row is a 44px target with a 20px box (flow 16's shared lone-checkbox rule); each `.form-grid` falls to one field a row; the dialog's buttons stack with Hold back on top (ConfirmDialog). Measured on the replay at 390 (states `curriculum-edit--narrow`, Step 6.31): nothing sideways.
